## Regulatory Compliance & Infrastructure

We ensure all facilities meet standards set by local health and safety authorities, including:

- **Hygiene & Safety**: Adhering to regulations for fire safety and food handling.
- **Infrastructure**: Installing grease traps, antimicrobial surfaces, and non-slip flooring.
- **Segregated Preparation Areas**: Clearly defined zones (meat, seafood, vegetables) to prevent cross-contamination.
- **Workflow Optimization**: Structuring layouts to follow HACCP guidelines for safety and efficiency.
