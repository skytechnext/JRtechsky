ABOUT MEGCOOK

The intelligent technology from Canada

MEGCOOK is Weston’s high-end intelligent kitchen electricity brand. As a Canadian high-tech enterprise, it focuses on R&D and manufacturing. By using the advanced technology and design concept of North America, the company is dedicated to developing world-leading intelligent cooking robots, which combed Chinese culinary traditions. Due to labor-saving, good taste, fast, and efficiency, MEGCOOKcreated a portable cooking robot industry and changed the traditional way of cooking fundamentally. Besides, the company has already led a new standard era of the Chinese food chain and helped many chain catering enterprises to solve the standardization of taste completely.

MEGCOOK’s R&D team is led by Canadian registered industrial designer Jerry.S and adheres to the Canadian design, R&D, and production system. High-quality automation products of the company span the globe, covering high-speed rail, solar power, wind power, new energy vehicles, smart kitchen appliances, and many other high-tech fields. After continuous R&D investment in recent decades, MEGCOOK’s 4th intelligent cooking robot has launched to the market. The robot has passed the EU CE, Canada CSA, South Korea KC, and Australia SAA certifications and has been well sold in Asia, Europe, America, and Oceania. This product, which has successfully promoted the intellectualization and light cooking of the cooking industry, provides standardization of catering and cooking intelligent solutions for thousands of brands worldwide.
