1 · Purpose & scope

JR-Tech operates four interlocking F&B-engineering business lines on spreadsheets, WhatsApp and disconnected point tools. This project replaces those with a single Odoo 19 Enterprise platform spanning lead-to-cash, fabrication, rental, field service, chemical subscriptions and finance — Malaysia-compliant (SST, MyInvois) and AI-ready.

In scope

  • CRM & Sales across all four lines
  • Manufacturing (stainless-steel fabrication, MTO)
  • Inventory & Purchase
  • Rental of equipment & dishwashers
  • Field Service, Maintenance & Repair (incl. grease-trap / tanker)
  • Subscriptions (recurring chemical resupply)
  • Accounting: localized CoA, SST, MyInvois e-invoicing, analytic by line
  • Opening-balance & master-data migration
  • Reporting / dashboards & role-based training

Out of scope (this phase)

  • E-commerce / customer web portal storefront
  • Payroll & full HR (deferred to a later phase)
  • Manufacturing shop-floor IoT / machine integration
  • Multi-currency (MYR only at go-live)
  • Custom mobile app beyond standard Odoo mobile
  • Historical transaction migration beyond opening balances & open items

7 · Assumptions & constraints

  • Odoo 19 Enterprise on Odoo.sh (Custom plan) — per Odoo Platform.
  • Single company, single currency (MYR), single legal entity at go-live.
  • JR-Tech provides clean master data (products, customers, opening balances) on agreed templates.
  • Named business users available for workshops, UAT and training.
  • MyInvois TIN, digital certificate & LHDN onboarding completed by JR-Tech with TechNext guidance.
  • Customisation favours configuration / Studio over custom code.
