Data Migration Strategy

JR-Tech's history lives in spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and paper files. This is how we get it into Odoo 19 cleanly: the sources, the source→model→field mapping, the cleansing and reconciliation approach, and a cutover that never risks the live business.

Where the data lives today

Sources

  • Excel / Google Sheets — customer lists, price lists, quotes, rental logs, chemical resupply schedules, stock counts.
  • WhatsApp — customer enquiries, service requests, order confirmations, photos of jobs and equipment.
  • Paper — service reports, delivery orders, signed contracts, supplier invoices, fabrication drawings.
  • Point tools — standalone accounting/bookkeeping file, email inbox.

The migration philosophy

Migrate masters and open items, not the full transactional archive. Customers, suppliers, products, price lists, open quotes/orders, rental assets, active service contracts and opening financial balances come across. Closed history stays in a read-only archive and (for finance) as opening balances — keeping the new database fast and clean.

WhatsApp & paper are not bulk-migrated. They are triaged: active threads and open jobs are captured manually into Odoo; the rest is scanned/archived. New inbound flows are redirected into Odoo from day one.

Cleansing approach

1 · Profile

Assess

Profile each source for duplicates, blanks, inconsistent formats and orphan references before any load. Quantify the cleansing effort up front.

2 · Standardise

Normalise

Phone/address formats, currency, units of measure, product naming and category taxonomy aligned to the Odoo structure on the architecture page.

3 · Dedupe

De-duplicate

Merge duplicate customers/suppliers/products using deterministic keys (name+phone, default_code) with a human-reviewed exception list.

4 · Enrich

Complete

Fill gaps that Odoo needs — TIN/SST IDs for MyInvois, UoM, product categories — owned by JR-Tech data stewards, not guessed.

5 · Stage

External IDs

Assign stable external IDs so relationships (orders→customers, BoMs→products) survive re-imports and trial loads.

6 · Validate

Gate

Row counts, control totals and referential checks must pass before promotion to the next environment.

Migration timeline

Two full trial loads de-risk the real cutover. Each trial is validated and signed off before the next; the production load happens over a controlled freeze window.

Cutover & reconciliation

Cutover strategy

  1. Freeze — short business freeze on master-data edits; legacy goes read-only.
  2. Delta load — load any records created since the last trial extract.
  3. Opening balances — post the dated opening journal and inventory adjustment.
  4. Reconcile & sign-off — finance and ops confirm totals match before the system is declared live.
  5. Go-live — Odoo becomes the system of record; new transactions only in Odoo.
