Digital maturity scorecard

Reading the scorecard

Brand assets are the strongest dimension — the 2024 rebrand gives JR-Tech a clear, premium story. Everything that converts that story — findability, lead capture, content and marketing automation — scores low.

So-what: the brand is ready; the digital machine to monetise it is not. That is a fixable, high-ROI gap.

Competitor digital benchmark

Reading the benchmark

Against a modelled profile of typical Penang/MY commercial-kitchen competitors, JR-Tech's brand strength is ahead of the pack — but it trails on website conversion, SEO footprint, social activity and review volume, the dimensions that actually surface a supplier in a buyer's search.

The gap is closeable: competitors lead on presence, not capability. The 90-day backlog targets exactly the four bars where JR-Tech sits below the benchmark line.

So-what for the Odoo 19 build

Digital presence is not a side project — it is the front of the funnel the ERP measures and converts. Odoo's Website, CRM and Marketing modules let JR-Tech publish a real catalog, capture web enquiries straight into CRM, and nurture them with automation — turning a brochure site into a measurable pipeline that feeds Sales.
