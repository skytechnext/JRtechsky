Founder-led risk profile

Founder dependence is the dominant operating risk for an SME of this shape. The chart contrasts the concentration today against the target state after an ERP-enabled process rollout.

What the radar says

Knowledge, pricing authority and customer trust are concentrated in the founder. Process documentation and decision delegation are the weakest dimensions — exactly where institutional memory should live in a system, not a person.

So-what for Odoo: codifying quoting rules, price lists, BoMs and service SLAs into Odoo moves tacit founder knowledge into the system — the single most effective key-person de-risking lever available.

So-what for the Odoo 19 build

Leadership analysis turns into two design mandates: (1) the ERP must externalise founder knowledge — pricing, BoMs, service standards — so the business is resilient to a single absence; and (2) role-based access and approval workflows must let department leads operate autonomously while the founder retains visibility, not control of every transaction.
