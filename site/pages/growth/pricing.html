From line-item quoting to value-priced, margin-aware selling across all four lines.

JR-Tech leaves margin on the table every time a bundled, outcome-based job is priced like a box of parts. This page sets a coherent pricing architecture — cost-plus where it must be, value-based where it can be — for equipment sales and rental, chemical subscriptions and service contracts, and shows how Odoo 19 makes tiered, dynamic and contract pricing executable instead of aspirational.

Quotes are built in spreadsheets, discounts are granted by feel, and the cost of fabrication labour, delivery and after-sales rarely lands on the same page as the sell price. The result is unpredictable margin and a sales team that defaults to discount to close.

Cost-plus vs. value-based — choose by line

LineDefault basisWhyTypical gross margin band
Standard equipment resaleCost-plusPrice-transparent, distributor-comparable hardware12–20%
Custom fabricationValue-basedBespoke design + compliance; no clean comparator30–45%
Equipment rentalAsset-yieldPrice to recover capital + utilisation risk over term35–55% lifetime
Chemical resupplyValue/subscriptionOutcome (clean, compliant) + dispenser lock-in40–60%
Service & maintenanceTiered contractSLA & uptime value, not hourly labour45–65%
Principle: compete on cost-plus only where the customer can commodity-compare (standard boxes). Everywhere JR-Tech adds design, compliance, uptime or recurring outcome, price to value and defend the bundle.

Bundle margin waterfall — a turnkey kitchen job

Where the margin is made and lost

List price erodes through equipment discount and freight, then rebuilds through higher-margin fabrication, the installed-service wrap and the attached chemical subscription. The lesson: the box is the loss-leader; the wrap and the recurring tail are the profit.

So-what for Odoo: Odoo's margin-on-quote shows this waterfall live as the salesperson builds the quotation — discounts trigger approval rules, and the system nudges attachment of the service + chemical lines that actually carry the deal.

Rental vs. sale economics

Why rental can outperform an outright sale

An outright dishwasher sale books margin once. The same asset on a 36-month rental with bundled servicing and warewash chemicals returns the capital and compounds recurring margin — while keeping the customer inside JR-Tech's service and resupply orbit.

  • Sale: fast cash, one-time margin, relationship ends at warranty.
  • Rental: deferred payback, higher lifetime margin, sticky service + chemical pull-through.

Chemical subscription pricing

Move warewash, housekeeping, laundry and pool chemicals from ad-hoc orders to a dispenser-anchored subscription. Price per outcome (per-cover, per-room, per-pool-month), amortise dosing hardware into the rate, and auto-replenish on consumption.

TierModelIncludesIndicative basis
Pay-as-you-goPer-litre listChemicals onlyHighest unit price

Defend the foothold: the embedded dispenser is the switching cost. Price the subscription so a price-only challenger cannot dislodge it without replacing hardware and retraining staff.

Value-based pricing model — worked RM example

Cost-plus prices the input; value-based prices the outcome the customer would otherwise pay for in lost covers, failed audits and emergency call-outs. The model below sets the price floor at cost-plus, the ceiling at the customer's economic value, and JR-Tech's target between them. A single 120-seat hotel banquet kitchen makes it concrete.
