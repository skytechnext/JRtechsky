Growth & Strategy

Running the four lines as one operation — capacity, inventory, fabrication and field service.

JR-Tech's growth is gated less by demand than by operational throughput: a fabrication shop with lumpy utilisation, inventory tied up across four lines, and field crews dispatched by phone. This page sets the operations strategy — capacity planning, inventory optimisation, workshop throughput, field-service routing and a sales-and-operations planning (S&OP) rhythm — and quantifies the before/after KPI shift Odoo 19 makes possible.

Five operational levers

1

Capacity planning

Forecast fabrication and service demand against crew and machine hours; level-load the workshop to flatten the feast-or-famine cycle that wastes welder time.

2

Inventory optimisation

Reorder rules, ABC classification and min/max by line — free working capital trapped in slow-moving stock while protecting fast-moving parts and chemicals.

3

Workshop throughput

Work orders, BoMs and routings turn the fabrication shop from drawing-driven craft into a measurable make-to-order flow with WIP visibility.

4

Field-service routing

Geo-clustered scheduling, skills matching and mobile job closeout lift technician utilisation and first-time-fix rates.

5

S&OP rhythm

A monthly cadence that reconciles the sales pipeline with fabrication capacity, stock and service load — one plan across all four lines.

∑

One data spine

All five run on the same Odoo records, so a confirmed quote flows into capacity, stock and dispatch without re-keying.

WIP tracked] C --> QC[QC & compliance check] QC --> I[Install on site] I --> S[Handover + service contract]
Throughput gain: when each job carries a BoM and routing, the shop can sequence work to keep welders loaded, pre-stage materials, and quote realistic delivery dates — cutting idle time and the rework that quietly erodes fabrication margin.

Field-service routing & utilisation

From phone dispatch to planned routes

Manual dispatch sends crews back and forth across the region and leaves van-hours unbilled. Geo-clustered scheduling, preventive-maintenance batching and grease-trap/tanker route planning lift the share of the day spent on billable work.

  • Cluster jobs by area and day to cut windscreen time.
  • Match technician skill to job type (warewash vs. piping vs. grease trap).
  • Batch contracted PM visits to anchor each route.

Before / after operations KPIs

Indexed targets after the Odoo 19 operations build: higher fabrication utilisation and technician productivity, lower inventory days and quote-to- delivery lead time, and a measurable lift in first-time-fix.
