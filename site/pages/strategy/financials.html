So what for the Odoo build: the lowest-margin line (equipment) is the largest, and the highest-margin lines (service, fabrication, consulting) are capacity- or attach-rate-limited. Odoo's lever is mix-shift: raise the service/chemical attach rate on every equipment sale, lifting blended margin toward the 36% target without new customers.

P&L bridge — today's model to the post-ERP model

The same revenue base, re-cut for what the ERP changes. The post-ERP P&L is not a growth fantasy — it is today's P&L with ~RM 430k/yr of recovered margin, retained recurring revenue and avoided cost flowing to the bottom line, net of the ~RM 83k/yr recurring platform cost. The implementation outlay (RM 254k Year-1) is capitalised/expensed in transition, not in this steady-state view.

Sensitivity — annual benefit vs. user-adoption rate

The single biggest swing factor is not the technology — it is how thoroughly staff actually use it. The RM 430k benefit assumes ~90% effective adoption of the quoting, subscription and field-service workflows. The grid below flexes the realised benefit (and the resulting payback) against adoption; even at a cautious 60% the case clears.

So what: the dominant lever on ROI is change management, not licence cost. This is why the engagement front-loads training, hypercare and role-based enablement — see Change Management.
