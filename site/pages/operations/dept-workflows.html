Operations

Current-state workflows — where the work actually breaks.

Six end-to-end processes carry JR-Tech's revenue: quote-to-cash, fabrication jobs, equipment rental, field service, chemical resupply and procurement. Below, each is mapped as it runs today — across spreadsheets, WhatsApp and phone calls — with the friction points flagged in red. These are the exact handoffs the Odoo 19 design eliminates.

Reading these maps

Steps shaded red are friction points — manual re-keying, lost context, missing data or uncontrolled approvals. Each diagram ends with the system of record today and the Odoo app that replaces it.

Cross-cutting friction: every process below depends on information living in a different place from where the next person works — so handoffs are re-typed, delayed, or simply forgotten. Detail in the Pain → Solution Matrix.

1 · Quote-to-cash (sales)

The spine of the business: enquiry → quote → order → delivery → invoice → payment, spanning all four business lines.

flowchart TD A([Enquiry via call / WhatsApp]) --> B[Salesperson logs lead in notebook] B --> C{Quote needed?} C -->|Yes| D[Rebuild quote in Excel from scratch]:::pain D --> E[Guess fabrication / install price]:::pain E --> F[Email PDF quote] F --> G{Customer accepts?} G -->|Negotiate| D G -->|Yes| H[Verbal handoff to ops]:::pain H --> I[Deliver / build / install] I --> J[Tell admin to invoice]:::pain J --> K[Manual invoice + SST in Excel]:::pain K --> L([Chase payment by phone]):::pain classDef pain fill:#FBE3E1,stroke:#C13B33,color:#7A211B;

System of record today: Excel + email + memory. Odoo replaces with: CRM → Sales → Accounting, one continuous record.

2 · Fabrication job (workshop)

Custom stainless-steel kitchen builds from the in-house fabrication team.

flowchart TD A([Won fabrication order]) --> B[Sketch / measure on site] B --> C[Estimate steel & labour by feel]:::pain C --> D[Buy material ad-hoc]:::pain D --> E[Cut · weld · assemble] E --> F[Track progress on paper job card]:::pain F --> G{Material short?} G -->|Yes| D G -->|No| H[Quality check] H --> I[Deliver & install] I --> J[Actual cost never reconciled to quote]:::pain J --> K([Margin unknown]):::pain classDef pain fill:#FBE3E1,stroke:#C13B33,color:#7A211B;

System of record today: paper job cards. Odoo replaces with: Manufacturing (BoM + routing + work orders) with real material & labour cost per job.

3 · Equipment rental

Sales & rental of kitchen equipment and dishwashers — an asset-lifecycle process today managed almost entirely off-system.

System of record today: rental notebook. Odoo replaces with: Rental + Inventory — asset tracking, automated return reminders, contract billing.

5 · Chemical resupply

Recurring dishwasher, housekeeping, laundry and pool chemical replenishment.

6 · Procurement

Buying steel, equipment, spares and chemicals to feed the other five flows.

flowchart TD A([Someone realises stock is low]):::pain --> B[Phone or WhatsApp a known vendor]:::pain B --> C[Compare price from memory / old emails]:::pain C --> D[Place verbal PO]:::pain D --> E[Goods arrive — no goods-receipt check]:::pain E --> F[Invoice filed in a tray]:::pain F --> G{Matches order?} G -->|Unclear| H[Pay anyway]:::pain G -->|Yes| I[Pay] H --> J([No spend visibility]):::pain I --> J classDef pain fill:#FBE3E1,stroke:#C13B33,color:#7A211B;

System of record today: emails + invoice tray. Odoo replaces with: Purchase (RFQ → PO → 3-way match) driven by Manufacturing demand & reorder rules.

Friction concentration by process

Counting the red steps above gives a crude but honest heat reading of where manual effort and risk concentrate today — and where Odoo automation pays back first.

So what for the Odoo build: quote-to-cash and field service carry the most friction and touch the most stakeholders, so they anchor the early phases. The future-state designs are on BPMN · Blueprint · UML.
