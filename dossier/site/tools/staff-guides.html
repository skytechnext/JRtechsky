Staff Guides — your daily tasks in Odoo, by role

One-page quick-starts for each JR-Tech role: what you do every day in Odoo 19, the exact steps, and tips. Pick your role below. These guides match the seeded Demo Walkthrough and the access rights set up by the AI Build Playbook.

🧑‍💼

Sales Rep

You turn enquiries into confirmed orders across all four business lines. Apps: CRM, Sales.

Daily tasks

  1. Check your pipeline. CRM → My Pipeline. Review new leads, update stages, set the expected close date and the business line tag.
  2. Qualify a lead. Open the opportunity, log the call/site visit in the chatter, add the customer contact details.
  3. Create a quotation. Click New Quotation (or Sales → New). Pick the customer, add product lines from the right catalogue, confirm the MYR pricelist and that SST shows.
  4. Send it. Send by Email — the branded PDF goes to the customer; the quote is version-tracked in the chatter.
  5. Confirm won deals. When accepted, Confirm. Fabrication quotes auto-create a manufacturing order; rental/subscription quotes start their cycles.
  6. Follow up. Use Activities to schedule the next touch; nothing slips.
Tip: never edit prices line-by-line — fix the pricelist so every rep quotes the same. Use Optional Products to upsell maintenance or chemicals on equipment deals.
🏭

Workshop / Fabrication Lead

You turn confirmed fabrication orders into finished stainless-steel products. Apps: Manufacturing, Inventory.

Daily tasks

  1. Review the MO list. Manufacturing → Operations → Manufacturing Orders. Sort by deadline; see which orders are ready (components available) vs waiting.
  2. Check material availability. Open an MO; if components are missing, the status shows Waiting — flag the storekeeper to replenish.
  3. Plan work orders. Move the job through the work centers: Cutting → Welding → Assembly → QC. Start/stop the timer on each operation.
  4. Produce. When QC passes, Mark as Done / Produce; the finished good enters stock and the linked sale order's delivery becomes ready.
  5. Report issues. Log scrap or rework so cost roll-up stays accurate.
Tip: keep BoMs current — if a design changes, update the BoM so material cost and the quote-vs-actual margin stay honest.
🛠

Field-Service Technician

You run service visits — grease-trap/tanker, installs, repairs — from your phone. App: Field Service (Odoo mobile).

Daily tasks

  1. See today's jobs. Open the Field Service app on mobile → My Tasks. Tasks are sorted by schedule and location.
  2. Travel & start. Tap a task, tap Start to begin the timer when you arrive on site.
  3. Do the work. Run the checklist, add parts used (they pull from van/store stock), and note anything for follow-up.
  4. Get sign-off. Capture the customer's signature, then Mark as Done / Validate.
  5. Invoice. The logged time + parts flow to an invoice automatically — no paperwork back at the office.
Tip: always start and stop the timer — billable hours you don't log are revenue JR-Tech never sees. Add photos to the task for proof of service.
📦

Storekeeper

You keep stock accurate and goods moving. Apps: Inventory, Purchase.

Daily tasks

  1. Process receipts. Inventory → Receipts. Validate incoming deliveries from vendors so stock updates immediately.
  2. Process deliveries. Inventory → Delivery Orders. Pick, pack and validate outgoing orders (sales, rentals, subscription resupplies).
  3. Handle rental pickup/return. Validate the pickup picking when an asset goes out and the return picking when it comes back.
  4. Watch replenishment. Check Reordering Rules; when a chemical or spare hits its min, confirm the auto-generated RFQ/PO.
  5. Count & reconcile. Run cycle counts via Inventory Adjustments to keep on-hand accurate.
Tip: validate movements the moment they happen — real-time stock is what lets sales promise delivery dates and the workshop start jobs.
📒

Finance

You own the ledger, SST and MyInvois. App: Accounting.

Daily tasks

  1. Review draft invoices. Accounting → Customer Invoices. Check tax (Sales 10% / Service 8%) and analytic line, then Confirm to post.
  2. Submit MyInvois. Posted B2B invoices submit to LHDN; watch the e-invoice status column and re-submit any failures.
  3. Enter/validate vendor bills. Match bills to POs & receipts (3-way), then post and schedule payment.
  4. Reconcile the bank. Use the Reconciliation screen daily so the close isn't a month-end scramble.
  5. Chase receivables. Run Follow-up reports; send reminders to overdue customers.
  6. Report. Pull P&L by analytic (business line), the SST report and AR aging for the Director.
Tip: keep periods locked once closed (Accounting → Lock Dates) so posted numbers can't drift. Full design in the Accounting Overhaul.
⚙️

Administrator

You keep the system healthy and people correctly access-controlled. Apps: Settings, all.

Daily / periodic tasks

  1. Manage users. Settings → Users. Add/disable staff, assign the right security groups per role (Sales, Inventory, Accounting, FSM, etc.).
  2. Check integrations. Confirm the MyInvois connection, outgoing mail and bank sync are healthy.
  3. Maintain master data. Keep products, pricelists, customers and vendors tidy; archive obsolete records rather than deleting.
  4. Apply config changes. Use Studio for small no-code tweaks; route bigger changes to TechNext under AMS.
  5. Monitor backups & staging. On Odoo.sh, verify daily backups and use the staging branch to test changes before production.
Tip: grant the least access a role needs. Field techs don't need accounting; sales don't need user management. The build playbook sets the baseline groups — keep to it.
