Scenario A · Fabrication quote → MO → delivery → invoice

The flagship JR-Consulting/fabrication flow: a Penang hotel orders a custom stainless-steel worktable, which is manufactured to order, delivered and invoiced with SST.

  1. Sales app → quotation. You see a draft quote for Eastin Hotel Penang, line item Custom SS Worktable 1800×700, qty 2, MYR pricelist, Sales Tax 10% line.
    Records: crm.lead "Eastin — kitchen fit-out" → sale.order S00012 (state draft).
  2. Confirm the quote. Click Confirm; the order becomes a sales order and a smart button "Manufacturing" appears.
    Records: sale.order S00012 → sale; a mrp.production is generated because the product is route = Manufacture/MTO.
  3. Manufacturing app → the MO. You see MO/00007 with its BoM components (SS sheet, frame tube, fasteners) and operations across work centers Cutting → Welding → Assembly → QC.
    Records: mrp.production MO/00007 + mrp.bom, stock.move (component consumption).
  4. Produce. Mark the work orders done and Produce; finished worktables enter stock at the workshop location.
    Records: MO/00007 → done; stock.move finished goods in.
  5. Delivery. Back on S00012, the Delivery smart button shows WH/OUT/00021; validate it to ship to the hotel.
    Records: stock.picking WH/OUT/00021 → done.
  6. Invoice + MyInvois. Create invoice → Confirm; the customer invoice carries the 10% Sales Tax and, once posted, shows a MyInvois submission status.
    Records: account.move INV/2026/0034 (state posted) with SST tax line + e-invoice status.

Scenario B · Equipment rental cycle

JR-Equipment Supports rents a commercial dishwasher to a café for two weeks — pickup, return and duration-based billing.

  1. Rental app → new order. You see a rental order for Hailam Kopi Café, product Commercial Dishwasher (Rental Asset), rental period 14 days with a price from the rental pricing rule.
    Records: sale.order R00005 with a rental line + sale.temporal.recurrence (duration).
  2. Confirm & pick up. Confirm the order, then Pick-up; the asset is marked out and reserved against availability.
    Records: pickup stock.picking → done; asset qty reserved.
  3. Return. After 14 days, Return the dishwasher; Odoo computes the billable duration and any late/damage fee.
    Records: return stock.picking → done; rental line quantity confirmed.
  4. Invoice. Create the invoice for the rental duration; Service Tax 8% applies.
    Records: account.move posted, analytic = Equipment & Rental.

Scenario C · Field-service maintenance visit

A grease-trap / bio-treatment service call handled on the technician's mobile, invoicing logged time and parts.

  1. Field Service app → task. You see FSM Task "Grease-trap service — Sunway Carnival Food Court", scheduled and assigned to a technician.
    Records: project.task / fsm.task FSM/00013.
  2. On site (mobile). The tech opens the task on the Odoo mobile app, runs the checklist, starts the timer, and adds the part Bio-treatment dosing.
    Records: account.analytic.line (timesheet) + a product line for parts used.
  3. Customer sign-off. Capture the customer signature and mark the task done.
    Records: task → done; signature stored.
  4. Invoice. Create invoice turns logged time + parts into a customer invoice with Service Tax 8%; analytic = Service.
    Records: sale.order (auto) → account.move posted.

Scenario D · Chemical subscription resupply

JR-Chemical Supplies runs a monthly recurring resupply of dishwasher detergent to a central kitchen — auto-invoiced and auto-delivered each cycle.

  1. Subscriptions app → the subscription. You see an active subscription for Pulau Tikus Central Kitchen, plan Monthly Detergent Resupply, recurring monthly, next-invoice date set.
    Records: sale.order with sale.subscription.plan SUB00004.
  2. Cycle runs. On the recurring date, Odoo generates the invoice automatically (the demo runs it manually via Generate invoice).
    Records: account.move posted, Sales Tax 10%, analytic = Chemicals; MRR updates.
  3. Delivery. A delivery order is created for the detergent quantity and validated from the chemicals store.
    Records: stock.picking → done.
  4. Reporting. The Subscriptions dashboard shows the new MRR and churn metrics; the owner sees recurring revenue building.
    Records: subscription KPIs (MRR) recomputed.

The thread tying it together
