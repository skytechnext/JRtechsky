Accounting Overhaul — from month-end spreadsheets to a live ledger

How JR-Tech's finance function moves from an external bookkeeper and Excel close to a real-time Odoo 19 Accounting backbone: a localized Malaysian chart of accounts, correct SST taxes, MyInvois e-invoicing, automated AR/AP, bank reconciliation, analytic accounting by business line, management reporting, opening-balance migration and a redesigned month-end close.

1 · Current bookkeeping pain

Where it hurts today

  • Books kept off-system by an external bookkeeper; data re-keyed from invoices & bank statements.
  • Margin and cash position are invisible until ~3 weeks after month-end.
  • No split of profitability by business line — fabrication vs rental vs chemicals vs service all blended.
  • SST handled manually; risk of mis-coded sales vs service tax.
  • MyInvois e-invoicing not yet operationalised — a live compliance exposure.
  • AR follow-up is ad hoc; overdue debtors chased inconsistently.
  • Bank reconciliation is a manual end-of-month exercise.
Compliance risk: Malaysia's MyInvois e-invoicing mandate is rolling out by turnover band; B2B invoices must be cleared through LHDN. Manual processes cannot scale to this — automating it is the single biggest reason finance must move onto Odoo now.

4 · MyInvois e-invoicing flow

  1. Onboarding. JR-Tech registers on the MyTax / MyInvois portal, obtains its TIN and digital certificate; TechNext configures these in l10n_my_edi.
  2. Invoice creation. Finance posts a customer invoice as usual in Odoo (from Sales, Rental, Subscriptions or Field Service).
  3. Submission. Odoo transmits the e-invoice to LHDN; the document gets a validation status and a QR/UIN reference.
  4. Clearance & sharing. On success the invoice is cleared; the validated PDF (with QR) is shared with the customer.
  5. Monitoring. Finance watches the MyInvois status column; failures are re-submitted. Consolidated e-invoices handle B2C as permitted.

Cross-reference the security & compliance detail on the Security, Compliance & MyInvois page.

5 · Analytic accounting by business line

Why analytic, not just departments

JR-Tech's value is knowing which line makes money. Every invoice line, vendor bill and fabrication cost is tagged to an analytic account, so the P&L can be sliced by line without separate ledgers. Default analytic distributions are set on products and on field-service / rental / subscription orders so tagging is automatic.

This feeds the owner dashboard on KPIs & Benefits and supports the Financial Snapshot.
