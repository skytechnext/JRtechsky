The transformation thesis

From disconnected tools to one operating system

JR-Tech runs four interlocking businesses on spreadsheets, WhatsApp and disconnected point tools. Quotes, fabrication jobs, rentals, service visits, chemical resupply and accounting live in silos — so margin, stock and service SLAs are invisible until month-end.

The TechNext bet: a single Odoo 19 backbone with AI-assisted quoting, predictive maintenance and MyInvois-compliant e-invoicing turns JR-Tech's "one-stop" promise into an operational reality — and a defensible moat.
