Headcount distribution & capacity

The read: workshop and field-tech staff (the billable capacity) are the constraint. Workshop utilisation runs hot in project peaks while field techs lose hours to scheduling gaps and travel — both invisible without a system.

Span of control & FTE by function

Reading span of control

Below the Director, five leads each carry a workable span (6–12 direct reports). The true span-of-control problem sits one level up: every lead reports to a single apex, and cross-functional decisions (pricing, procurement) re-converge there.

The read: the org isn't over-layered — it's under-delegated. Odoo approval workflows widen the founder's effective span without adding management layers.

So-what for the Odoo 19 build

The org shape sets the ERP's role and scheduling design: five department roles with scoped access; Manufacturing work orders and timesheets for the workshop; Field Service dispatch, route and parts for technicians; Subscriptions for chemical logistics; and HR for a ~45-person establishment. The capacity story is the benefits case — utilisation, not headcount, is the lever.
