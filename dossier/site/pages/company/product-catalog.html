Line 3 · Stainless-steel fabricated items Make-to-Order · BoM

Fabricated items are configured-to-order. In Odoo each is a manufactured product with a Bill of Materials (sheet, profile, fittings) and routing (cut · form · weld · polish), produced via the MTO route triggered from the sales quote.

Why a BoM, not a flat product: margin on fabrication is decided at the shop floor. Costing the sheet, fittings and labour through a BoM is what makes project margin visible at quote time rather than at month-end.

Line 5 · Chemicals — four sub-lines Consumable · Subscription

Recurring by design

Chemicals are consumable and best sold on resupply. In Odoo they are modelled as consumable products attached to Subscription plans with reorder cadence per customer site.

So-what: subscriptions convert one-off chemical orders into predictable monthly recurring revenue — and feed demand forecasting for inventory.
