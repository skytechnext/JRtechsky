Sentiment themes (modelled)

What drives the score

For F&B-engineering SMEs, positive sentiment clusters around responsiveness, technical competence and one-stop convenience. Friction clusters around response time on service calls, parts availability and follow-up after install.

So-what: nearly every negative theme is an operations/SLA problem an ERP-backed service desk fixes — reputation is downstream of field execution.

Reputation-management playbook

An operating playbook with two halves: a review-generation engine that systematically turns good jobs into public proof, and response SLAs that ensure no feedback goes unanswered.

Review generation engine

  1. Trigger an automated review request 24h after every closed service/install job.
  2. Route satisfied customers (CSAT ≥ 4) to Google & Facebook; route the rest to a private recovery flow first.
  3. Quarterly outreach to repeat & referral accounts to convert private goodwill into public reviews and short case studies.
  4. Make leaving a review frictionless — QR on the job sheet and a one-tap link.

NPS-style model

A modelled promoter/passive/detractor split for a referral-strong but process-light SME. The headline NPS is positive but fragile — heavily dependent on the founder personally closing service loops.

Why it's fragile

Promoters are real but concentrated in relationship accounts. Detractors come from service-execution slips — late visits, repeat fixes, parts delays — that scale poorly as volume grows.

Lever: systematic SLAs and proactive maintenance convert passives to promoters and shrink the detractor tail.

Before / after reputation funnel

Reading the funnel

Today most goodwill evaporates between a happy job and a public review — no ask, no capture, no response. With the Odoo-driven playbook, every stage retains more: jobs trigger asks, asks convert to reviews, and reviews get answered and surfaced.

The shift: the firm's strongest asset — private referral trust — finally becomes public proof a new buyer can find. Volumes are illustrative of the conversion uplift, not measured counts.

So-what for the Odoo 19 build

Reputation is the output of operations, so the ERP is the reputation engine: Field Service SLAs and proactive Maintenance reduce the friction that creates detractors; automated post-job feedback requests build review volume and velocity; and Helpdesk cases make sure no complaint is dropped. Public trust then compounds into the demand the digital funnel captures.
