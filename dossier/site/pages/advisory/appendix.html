This dossier blends a small core of verified primary facts about JR-Tech Solution Sdn Bhd with publicly observable signals and a layer of TechNext analyst modelling where public data is unavailable. The split is summarised below and detailed in Methodology & Assumptions.

Methodology summary

The four-step approach

  1. Anchor on primary facts. Extract verified identity, contact, business-line and capability data from the 2024 JR-Tech brand book and the director's business card.
  2. Widen with public signals. Corroborate against the public website (www.jrtech.com.my), the Malaysian HORECA market and the Odoo 19 product surface.
  3. Model the gaps. Where headcount, revenue and named clients are not public, construct realistic Penang-SME estimates consistent with the primary facts.
  4. Tie every claim to the build. Convert each finding into an Odoo 19 design or delivery implication — the "so what" that the implementation plan acts on.
Single caveat, not scattered ones. Rather than footnoting every modelled number, we consolidate the modelling disclosure on the Methodology page. Treat any financial, headcount or named-client figure as a TechNext analyst model unless explicitly attributed to a primary source here.
