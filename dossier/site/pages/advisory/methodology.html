How the dossier was produced

The engagement followed a disciplined evidence pipeline: anchor on what is verifiably true about JR-Tech, widen with public signals, model only the gaps, then convert every finding into a concrete Odoo 19 design or delivery decision.

What is verified

Legal name and SSM number, incorporation era (~2009), director and contact details, the Gelugor (Penang) office, the four business lines, and the brand/AI positioning — all drawn directly from the brand book and business card.

What is modelled

Headcount, revenue and valuation, named clients, win-rates, shrinkage rates and the benefit/ROI figures in the interactive tools. These are realistic Penang-SME estimates, clearly indicative rather than audited.

The consolidated modelling note

Read this once; it applies everywhere. Where public data does not exist — specifically financials (revenue, margins, valuation), headcount, and client names — the figures in this dossier are TechNext analyst models, constructed to be realistic for a Penang-based F&B kitchen-engineering SME incorporated around 2009 and consistent with the verified facts on the Appendix & Sources page. They are decision-support estimates, not audited accounts, statutory filings, or guarantees of outcome. We deliberately state this once rather than caveating every number, so the analysis reads cleanly. Verified primary facts are attributed in the source list (S1–S7); anything not attributable there should be treated as modelled (S8).

Frameworks used

Each analytical section is built on an established framework so the reasoning is transparent and repeatable rather than ad hoc.

Macro

PESTLE

Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental scan of the MY F&B-engineering context.

Position

SWOT / TOWS

Internal strengths/weaknesses against external opportunities/threats, converted to action via TOWS.

Industry

Porter's Five Forces

Rivalry, entrants, substitutes, and buyer/supplier power in commercial-kitchen supply.

Process

BPMN & UML

As-is/to-be process modelling and data/object modelling for the Odoo design.

Value

Value-Chain Analysis

Primary and support activities mapped to capability and to Odoo modules.

Delivery

RACI & Risk Register

Accountability mapping and structured risk scoring across the rollout.

Finance

TCO / NPV / ROI

Discounted three-year cost-benefit modelling underpinning the ROI/TCO tool.

Benefit

Driver-based benefit modelling

Time, win-rate and shrinkage drivers translated into the Profit Estimator.

Personas

Archetype & JTBD

Customer archetypes and jobs-to-be-done framing the demand-side analysis.
