Reading the urgency profile

Urgency scored 1–5 by TechNext against two tests: is value being destroyed today (the fire-exposed, invisible stock), and does it gate other decisions (OKRs frame the whole engagement; headcount re-bases the quote; KL plans fix the architecture).

The two red items move this month: an OKR/KPI workshop because it is the owner's own stated priority, and a barcode/inventory pilot because the next fire — or the next uncounted shrinkage — is uninsurable without records. Everything else sequences behind them without blocking the implementation roadmap.
