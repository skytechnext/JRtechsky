Kitchen Workflow — JR-Tech Method

The First Meeting: 90 minutes that replace six weeks

Everything downstream — the blueprint, the SOPs, the projections, the quotation — is generated from what this one meeting captures. So the meeting is engineered like an instrument: four inputs, each with a defined checklist, recorded straight into Odoo before JR-Tech leaves the site. If the capture is complete, the engine can run. If it isn't, the scorecard says so before anyone promises a proposal date.

The four inputs — and exactly what we capture

Input 1

Kitchen floor plans

Architect's drawings, landlord's as-built plans, or — if none exist — JR-Tech measures and sketches on site. These become the canvas for the entire Blueprint Pack.

What we captureWhy the engine needs it
Overall dimensions & ceiling heightLayout envelope, hood clearances
Structural columns & load-bearing wallsImmovable constraints on equipment lines
Ventilation shafts & riser locationsExhaust routing, smokeless-cooking feasibility
Existing gas lines & meter positionGas vs. induction decision per station
Existing M&E (DB boards, plumbing, floor traps)Reuse vs. new electrical & drainage points
Doors, corridors & lift dimensionsFabrication panel sizes, delivery path
Input 2

Menu

The owner's draft menu — even a rough one — drives menu engineering, station design and the revenue model. No menu, no meaningful kitchen.

What we captureWhy the engine needs it
Full dish list & cuisine typeCooking methods → equipment classes
Expected peak covers per serviceThroughput sizing, projected daily output
Prep complexity per dish (mise en place depth)Prep-area sizing, staffing & SOPs
Target price points & average ticketRevenue projection, food-cost targets
Hero dishes & batch-cook candidatesCook-and-chill opportunity, hold equipment
Input 3

Restaurant owner's requests

The commercial and brand constraints. Captured verbatim in the owner's words, then structured into fields the engine can optimise against.

What we captureWhy the engine needs it
Total budget & CAPEX vs. rental preferenceEquipment schedule tiering, rental scenarios
Seating capacity & service styleKitchen-to-front ratio, pass design
Target opening dateFabrication & delivery timeline feasibility
Brand vision & guest experience goalsOpen vs. closed kitchen, show elements
Dietary focus (halal, vegetarian, allergens)Segregated prep areas, compliance layout
Input 4

JR-Tech site inspection notes

The input only JR-Tech can produce: an engineer's structured survey of what the premises can physically support. This is where sixteen years of M&E judgement enters the data.

What we captureWhy the engine needs it
Electrical supply: single/three-phase, available amperageInduction line feasibility, load calculations
Water pressure & incoming pipe sizeDishwasher & combi-oven specification
Drainage falls & grease-trap positionDrainage points plan, bio-treatment sizing
Floor loading & finish conditionHeavy equipment placement (cold rooms, combis)
Exhaust routes & makeup-air optionsHood design, smokeless-tech alternatives
Access for equipment delivery (stairs, lifts, timing)Installation instructions in the JR-Tech Pack
Capture discipline: each checklist row is a required field on the Odoo site-survey form (a Studio model on the CRM lead). The tablet form will not submit with blanks — which is precisely what makes the 72-hour turnaround promise safe. The master question bank lives in Discovery Questions.

The 72 hours after the meeting

  1. Hour 0–2 — Data lands. All four inputs are attached to the Odoo lead before the team leaves site; the completeness scorecard is stamped on the record.
  2. Hour 2–24 — Engine run. AI actions perform floorplan analysis, menu engineering and electrical/water load calculations; draft equipment schedule and annotated floorplan are generated against JR-Tech's live catalog and price lists.
  3. Hour 24–48 — Engineering review. JR-Tech consultants validate clearances, loads, drainage falls and fabrication feasibility; the operations and financial drafts are tuned (staffing, SOPs, projections — same models as the Profit Estimator).
  4. Hour 48–72 — Pack assembly. The four packs and the quotation are compiled into the full proposal; the presentation meeting is confirmed with the owner.

Data-completeness scorecard

Green means go

The scorecard is reviewed live in the last ten minutes of the meeting. Each input area is scored by required-field coverage on the Odoo forms. The engine only runs automatically at 90%+ overall; below that, the consultant books a specific follow-up (usually a missing drawing or an unconfirmed budget) rather than letting the 72-hour clock start on bad data.

Input areaTypical first-pass scoreCommon gap
Floor plans92%Missing ceiling-void detail for exhaust
Menu78%Peak covers not yet decided
Owner requests95%Budget range instead of ceiling
Site inspection98%Landlord confirmation of amperage upgrade
So what for the Odoo build: the scorecard is a computed field on the CRM lead — one Studio model, four form views, one automated action gating the AI pipeline. Scoped in the Requirements (BRD).
