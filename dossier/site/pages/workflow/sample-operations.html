Scope of this pack

This pack operationalises the approved Kitchen Blueprint (ref. JRT-2026-AGL-01, Blueprint Pack R1: RM185,000 equipment schedule, 7-week build). It assumes the six-station layout as installed — wok line (2 woks under the smokeless hood), simmer/stock, steam, cold prep/kerabu, dessert/plating, dishwash return — and a normal trading day of 130 covers ≈ RM12,350 at an average check of RM95. Every SOP, policy and schedule below is delivered as a live record in the client's Odoo workspace under the JR-Tech recurring service (RM2,980/mo), so the document you are reading is a snapshot, not the system.

R-1 · Pre-batch Kapitan rempah

Curry Kapitan and Gulai Tumis share a rempah base. Batch it twice weekly at the simmer station (SOP-04) and hold chilled in E-07; wok finish drops from 14 min to 6 min per order at peak.

R-2 · Push Cendol as the star

20% food cost and near-universal uptake make Cendol the highest-velocity margin item. Print it on every table tent and train servers to offer it by default; the E-11 station runs it with zero wok-line load.

R-3 · Prep-ahead kerabu

Kerabu Bee Hoon components (toasted coconut, herbs, dressing) prep fully by 10:00 and assemble to order in under 3 minutes at E-06 — it becomes the pressure-relief dish when the wok line queues.

SOP-03 · RECEIVING & FIFO STORAGEOwner: Cold prep cook · Daily 08:30–09:30 · Rev R1
  1. Stage & verify. Receive at the back-lane door only. Check every line against the Odoo purchase order — quantity, grade, and supplier seal intact.
  2. Temperature check. Probe chilled goods on arrival: reject anything above 5°C (fish, poultry, coconut milk). Frozen goods must show no thaw drip. Record readings in the Odoo quality check.
  3. Sensory check. Fish: clear eyes, firm flesh, sea smell only. Fresh herbs (daun kesum, bunga kantan) crisp and unbruised — kerabu quality dies here, not at the plate.
  4. Label. Date-label every item on receipt: received date + use-by. No unlabelled container enters E-06/E-07/E-08.
  5. FIFO placement. New stock behind or below old stock, never in front. Raw proteins on the lowest shelf; ready-to-eat above.
  6. Put away within 20 minutes. Chilled goods must be inside a chiller within 20 minutes of arrival; log the put-away time.
  7. Close the loop. Validate the receipt in Odoo; rejected lines trigger an automatic supplier credit-note request.
Critical limits: receive chilled ≤ 5°C · frozen fully frozen · put-away ≤ 20 min · any rejection photographed and logged before the driver leaves. Two rejections from one supplier in 30 days escalates to a JR-Tech sourcing review.

Safety, hygiene & policies

The pack adopts HACCP critical control points scaled to a single-kitchen operation, plus the Malaysian statutory requirements that inspectors actually check in George Town.

5.1 HACCP critical control points

Cooling60°C → 21°C within 2 hSteam-simmer cook, blast-portion in shallow pansDiscard if limit missed; log incident

5.2 Personal hygiene policy

  • Clean uniform and apron daily; aprons off before toilet or waste runs.
  • Hand wash on entry, after raw protein handling, every 30 minutes on the cold line.
  • No jewellery below the elbow; hair fully covered; nails short and unpolished.
  • Cuts covered with blue detectable plasters plus a glove.
  • Staff with vomiting or diarrhoea excluded 48 h symptom-free before return.

5.3 Malaysia statutory requirements

  • Food-handler training (LPM): every crew member completes the KKM-recognised Latihan Pengendali Makanan course; certificates stored in Odoo HR with expiry alerts.
  • TY2 typhoid vaccination: valid for all food handlers; injection dates and 3-year renewals tracked in Odoo HR.
  • MBPP premises licence and grease-trap discharge compliance (SOP-10) kept current; inspection folder exportable from Odoo in one click.
