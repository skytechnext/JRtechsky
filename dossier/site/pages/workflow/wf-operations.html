1 · Recommended menu engineering

The founding rule of the JR-Tech method: the menu drives the equipment — never the other way round. Selat 88's owner arrived with 68 dish ideas; the engine scored each for margin, prep load and equipment dependency, and returned a 32-dish opening menu every station can execute at full house without a bottleneck. A dish that needs a salamander the blueprint doesn't include simply doesn't make the cut — or the blueprint is revised first.

How the engine reads the matrix

  • Stars (high margin, high popularity) — anchor the menu, guarantee their equipment (wok, steamer, live tanks) has zero single-point queue at peak.
  • Plowhorses (popular, thinner margin) — re-cost portions and prep batching; fried rice moved to a dedicated second wok burner at peak.
  • Puzzles (profitable, unpopular) — reposition on the menu page and train servers to suggest; grilled prawns become a set-menu feature.
  • Dogs — cut or restrict. The abalone claypot survives only as a pre-order banquet item, so its slow-braise never blocks the stock stove on a Friday night.

The scoring re-runs monthly from Odoo POS sales data, so the menu keeps earning its equipment. Menu changes feed back into the Blueprint Pack revision loop before any new appliance is bought.

People policies (Malaysia requirements built in)

  • Food-handler certification: every kitchen employee completes the KKM-recognised Latihan Pengendali Makanan (LPM) course before first shift; certificate stored on the employee record in Odoo HR.
  • Typhoid vaccination (injeksi tifoid): valid TY2 vaccination is mandatory for all food handlers; expiry dates tracked in Odoo with 60-day renewal alerts — an expired jab blocks the roster.
  • Personal hygiene policy: clean uniform daily, hair restraint, no jewellery on line, hand-wash on entry and every task change, cuts covered with blue plasters, unwell staff (vomiting/diarrhoea) excluded 48h symptom-free.
  • Incident reporting: injuries, customer complaints and suspected food-poisoning reports logged within 2h as Odoo incidents; anything notifiable escalated to the owner and, where required, MBPP health office same day.
  • Visitor & contractor rule: no entry past the staff line without a hygiene briefing; JR-Tech technicians follow the same rule on service visits.

Roster logic

Kitchen labour is rostered to forecast covers, not habit. Odoo POS history drives a day-by-day cover forecast; the chart shows the peak-week pattern the roster is built against — a lean 8-person Tuesday line rising to the full 11-person brigade Friday to Sunday. Monday is the deep-clean and maintenance day precisely because it is the trough. Payroll target: kitchen labour ≤ 13% of revenue.
