1 · Zoned floorplan concept — Restoran Selat 88

The layout enforces a one-way food-safety flow: raw goods move forward only — receiving to storage to prep to the hot line to the pass — while soiled ware returns on a separate dishwash loop that never crosses the raw-to-cooked path. Staff and waste have their own routes. This is the layout logic MBPP health inspectors look for.

Cross-contamination controls designed in

  • No return path for raw goods — solid arrows flow one way; only clean ware and soiled ware move on the dashed dishwash loop.
  • Segregated prep sinks — separate seafood and vegetable sinks with colour-coded boards; hand-wash basins at prep entry and hot-line entry.
  • Waste never crosses the pass — bins exit via the waste room beside receiving, on the opposite side from plating.
  • Halal-ready option — the same footprint supports a fully segregated variant (dedicated store shelving, utensil colour system, no cross-use of fryers) if Selat 88 later pursues JAKIM certification.

4 · Ventilation, exhaust & gas notes

Exhaust & make-up air

  • Hood E15 sized at 4.2m to overhang the hot line 300mm each side; design capture velocity 0.5 m/s, ~9,500 m³/h extract.
  • Baffle filters (not mesh) for wok-load grease; filter racks sized to fit the dishwasher for weekly wash-through.
  • Make-up air ≈ 85% of extract, ducted low-level so the dining room stays positive-pressure (no smells into the 120-seat floor).
  • Duct route to roof shown on the DWG; access doors every 3m for cleaning. An ecology unit (ESP + carbon) option is priced if MBPP requires it for the specific lot's discharge point.

LPG system

  • External cylinder bank (6 × 50kg, auto changeover) in a ventilated cage — never inside the kitchen.
  • Copper/SS manifold at 2.8 kPa to the line; individual appliance isolation valves.
  • Gas–fan interlock: solenoid shuts gas if the exhaust fan stops. Emergency cut-off button at the kitchen exit.
  • Piping installed and pressure-tested by a Suruhanjaya Tenaga-registered gas contractor; test certificate filed in the Odoo project for the BOMBA submission.

6 · Revision workflow — 48-hour re-issue

  1. Owner marks up — on the PDF, on paper, or in a 30-minute call. Markups are logged as tasks in the shared Odoo project.
  2. Engine re-flows — layout, equipment schedule, point schedule, load summary and budget update together from the single model; the AI flags any knock-on effect (e.g. moving the fryer changes hood length and gas run).
  3. Re-issue in 48 hours — Rev B PDF/DWG/XLSX with a change log; superseded drawings are archived, never deleted.
  4. Sign-off — owner approves in the Odoo portal; the approved revision becomes the contract drawing set and the basis of the fabrication work orders.
Why 48 hours is possible: conventional consultants redraw by hand across disconnected files. The JR-Tech engine keeps one model in Odoo, so a revision is a re-generation, not a redraw. Two revision cycles are included; further cycles are quoted transparently.
