Why the dinner turn carries the model

Dinner delivers 64% of a normal day's revenue on 55% of the covers — seafood check averages climb sharply after 18:00 (live tanks, steamed fish, crab). The kitchen is therefore engineered around the 19:00–20:30 dinner peak: roughly 90 covers/hour, which at Selat 88's 2.4 dishes-per-cover menu mix means 216 dishes/hour leaving the pass.

So what for the Odoo build: the daypart model is loaded as budget lines in Odoo Accounting and as demand curves in Planning, so post-opening POS actuals can be compared to this exact projection, daypart by daypart.

The constraint is deliberate

The wok line runs at 89% of rated capacity at the dinner peak — the highest of any station, and by design. Woks are the most expensive station in capex, power and skilled labour; sizing it to 100%+ headroom would add ~RM48k of equipment that idles 20 hours a day. Instead, the Operations Pack shifts 15% of wok demand to cook-and-chill: sauces and stocks are batch-produced at tea-time, blast-chilled, and regenerated at dinner — flattening the peak without more woks.

If Selat 88 later outgrows this, one additional induction wok drops into a pre-provisioned 15 kW power point already shown on the M&E drawing.

An honest number, not a brochure number

Honesty note: the savings above are modelled estimates, not guarantees. They are validated against the actual equipment schedule (nameplate loads × measured diversity factors from comparable JR-Tech installations), current TNB commercial tariffs, and Penang F&B wage benchmarks. Actual results depend on menu mix, operating hours and staff discipline. Post-opening, Odoo dashboards replace the model with metered actuals — see section E.

Cumulative 5-year savings: energy + gas ≈ RM 447k, labour ≈ RM 576k gross; after a prudent 20% realisation haircut on labour, the modelled total is ≈ RM 908k. The equipment-price premium of the JR-Tech build (~RM 62k) pays back in under 5 months.

E · How these numbers stay live in Odoo

Projection → dashboard, same structure

  1. Budgets loaded at signing. Every line of the P&L above becomes an analytic budget in Odoo Accounting under the "Selat 88 — Kitchen" analytic plan.
  2. POS actuals flow in daily. Covers, check and daypart revenue from the POS land against the same daypart dimensions used in section A.
  3. Metered energy replaces the model. The kitchen's sub-meter reading is logged monthly on the JR-Tech maintenance visit (a Field Service work-order step), so modelled kWh becomes measured kWh.
  4. Variance alerts. Food cost drifting >2 pts above 34%, or kWh >10% above model, triggers an activity for both the owner and JR-Tech's account manager.
