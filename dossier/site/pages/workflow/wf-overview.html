The old way vs. the JR-Tech Method

For sixteen years JR-Tech has planned, fabricated and equipped commercial kitchens across Penang. The bottleneck was never capability — it was the workflow. The traditional kitchen-fit-out cycle forces the owner to be the general contractor of their own planning.

The old way

Weeks of back-and-forth, quote covers equipment only

  • Owner sends a floor plan; JR-Tech replies with an equipment list and price.
  • Every layout revision means another site visit and another week of email.
  • The quote is equipment only — no menu engineering, no SOPs, no staffing plan, no revenue projection.
  • The owner separately assembles an interior designer, an M&E contractor, a food-safety consultant and an accountant — each working from different drawings.
  • Decisions stall because nobody can answer "what will this kitchen actually earn?"
The new way

One meeting → the full kitchen + restaurant business plan

  • One 90-minute structured meeting captures four inputs: floor plans, menu, owner's requests, and JR-Tech's own site-inspection notes.
  • The AI + Odoo engine converts those inputs into ~20 deliverables in four packs within days.
  • The proposal the owner signs already contains the equipment schedule, the annotated floorplan, the SOPs, the financial projections and JR-Tech's delivery timeline.
  • Signing the agreement = instant handover to execution. Fabrication, procurement and installation start from documents that already exist in Odoo.
The reframe: JR-Tech stops selling equipment quotes and starts selling a de-risked restaurant launch. The equipment is still in the deal — but it arrives wrapped in the plan that proves it will pay for itself. Time compression: 6–8 weeks becomes 5–7 days

Where the weeks go — and where they don't

In the traditional cycle, elapsed time is dominated by waiting: waiting for revised drawings, waiting for third-party consultants, waiting for a re-quote after every scope change. The JR-Tech Method removes the waiting, not the thinking — capture is compressed into one meeting, and analysis runs in parallel inside the engine instead of sequentially across firms.

Why it works

Expertise

Sixteen years of floorplan & M&E judgement

JR-Tech's consulting team — space planning, in-house stainless fabrication, electrical and drainage layout experience since 2009 — is what trains and constrains the engine. The AI proposes; JR-Tech's engineers validate every load calculation and clearance before anything reaches the owner.

Data

Odoo as the single source of truth

Product catalog, price lists, supplier lead times, fabrication BoMs and past project actuals all live in Odoo 19. The engine doesn't guess equipment prices or install durations — it reads them. That is why the quotation and the projection agree with each other.

AI

AI does the assembly, not the judgement

Floorplan analysis, menu engineering, load calculations and document generation are pattern work at machine speed. The AI & Automation Catalog maps each step to a concrete Odoo automation; the Why AI page makes the strategic case.

So what for the Odoo build: this workflow is the flagship use-case of the implementation. It requires CRM (lead intake), Documents (input capture), Studio models (site-survey data), Sales (quotation), Project (execution handover) and the AI action layer — all scoped in the Requirements (BRD).
