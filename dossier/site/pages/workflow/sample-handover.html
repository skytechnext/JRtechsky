SECTION 1 Delivery & installation plan — 5 phases, 7 weeks

Fabrication runs in parallel at the JR-Tech workshop while the site is prepared, so the heritage building is only disrupted for the weeks that genuinely need it. The restaurant continues trading until the end of week 2.

SECTION 5 Day-0 automation — signature to execution

Because the four packs were modelled in Odoo before signing, the signature is not the start of planning — it is the start of execution. The moment Auntie Gaik Lean's agreement completes in Odoo Sign, the following chain fires without anyone re-keying a number.

flowchart TD A["Agreement signed in Odoo Sign"] --> B["Sales order JRT-2026-AGL-01 confirmed"] B --> C["Project created with 7-week plan and 5 phases"] B --> D["Fabrication manufacturing orders released to workshop"] B --> E["Purchase orders issued to brand suppliers"] C --> F["Delivery schedule booked - night back-lane slots, week 3"] B --> G["Maintenance contract activated - RM1,180/mo"] B --> H["Chemical subscription activated - RM1,800/mo"] G --> I["12 months of Field Service PM work orders generated"] B --> J["Milestone invoice 1 issued - 40 percent, RM74,000"] C --> K["P and L budget lines loaded to Accounting for variance tracking"]
The punchline of the method: planning is done before signing, so execution starts the same day. On the afternoon of signature the workshop is cutting stainless, the brand POs are out, the week-3 delivery nights are booked, and the first milestone invoice is in the owner's inbox — with the 7-week clock already running.
