Who the ERP serves — and what each of them needs to win.

JR-Tech's "one-stop F&B engineering solutionist" promise is delivered by seven distinct stakeholder groups, each measured on different outcomes. This page maps their power and interest, then states — concretely — the goals, pains and the specific Odoo 19 capability that resolves each constituency. Every later design decision in this dossier traces back here.

The stakeholder landscape

A mid-sized Penang F&B engineering SME runs lean: the Director still touches most decisions, while four operating functions and two enabling functions carry the day-to-day. Customers sit on the outside but exert the strongest pull on every process.

Power / interest grid

Each bubble is a stakeholder placed by their influence over the engagement (power, y-axis) and their day-to-day stake in the platform (interest, x-axis). The quadrant dictates engagement strategy: manage closely (top-right), keep satisfied (top-left), keep informed (bottom-right) and monitor (bottom-left).

Keep informed

Engage

How the stakeholders connect

Value flows from a customer enquiry through sales into the operating functions, supported by procurement and finance. Today these handoffs happen over WhatsApp, phone and spreadsheets; Odoo turns each arrow into a tracked, auditable record.

Stakeholder cards — goals, pains, what Odoo gives them

Manage closely

Director — Tan Gim Boon

Goals: protect margin across four business lines, keep the "one-stop" reputation intact, make confident decisions without waiting for month-end.

Pains: no single view of cash, jobs or stock; decisions rely on memory and WhatsApp threads; profitability per job is invisible until it is too late.

Odoo gives: a live executive dashboard (Spreadsheet / Studio KPIs) with consolidated P&L, pipeline, work-in-progress and overdue receivables — one screen, real time.
Keep informed

Sales & Quoting

Goals: turn enquiries into accurate quotes fast, across consulting, equipment, rental and chemicals; win on responsiveness, not just price.

Pains: quotes rebuilt from scratch in Excel; no version history; pricing for fabricated items guessed; lost visibility once a job moves to the workshop.

Odoo gives: CRM + Sales with reusable product/BoM-priced quote templates, AI-assisted line suggestions, version control and one-click conversion to a manufacturing, rental or service order.
Keep informed

Workshop / Fabrication Lead

Goals: deliver custom stainless-steel kitchens on time and on cost; keep the welding team utilised; reduce rework and material waste.

Pains: jobs tracked on paper; no Bill of Materials, so steel and labour costs are estimated; no link between the quote and the shop floor.

Odoo gives: Manufacturing with BoMs, routings and work orders; real material consumption and labour booked against each job for true cost.
Engage

Field-Service Technicians

Goals: arrive prepared, fix first-time, log work cleanly; service installs, repairs, grease-trap, bio-treatment and tanker jobs across Penang & the Klang Valley.

Pains: jobs dispatched by phone; no asset history on site; parts not on the van; service reports handwritten and re-keyed (or lost).

Odoo gives: Field Service mobile app — scheduled jobs, equipment service history, parts on the order, photos and signed reports captured on the phone; Maintenance triggers preventive visits automatically.
Engage

Chemical Supply

Goals: keep recurring dishwasher, housekeeping, laundry and pool chemical customers replenished on time; grow predictable recurring revenue.

Pains: resupply driven by customer calls or memory; no consumption tracking; stock-outs and emergency deliveries erode margin.

Odoo gives: Subscriptions for auto-recurring resupply orders and invoices, plus Inventory reorder rules so chemical SKUs are never short.
Manage closely

Finance / Admin

Goals: bill accurately and fast, stay SST- and MyInvois-compliant, control cash, close the month quickly.

Pains: double entry between Excel and accounting; manual SST; e-invoicing looming; receivables chased ad-hoc; no job-level profitability.

Odoo gives: Accounting with automated SST, MyInvois e-invoice submission, analytic accounting per job/line, and aged-receivable follow-ups.
Engage

Procurement

Goals: buy steel, equipment, spares and chemicals at the right price and time; avoid both stock-outs and dead stock.

Pains: purchasing reactive and manual; no link to fabrication BoMs or reorder points; vendor pricing scattered across emails.

Odoo gives: Purchase with RFQs, vendor price lists and automated reordering driven by manufacturing demand and inventory rules.
Keep satisfied

Customers (HORECA)

Goals: hotels, restaurants, central kitchens, hospitals and condos want one reliable partner for design, equipment, service and chemicals — with no surprises.

Pains: unclear quote status, slow service response, no record of past work, paper invoices.

Odoo gives: a customer Portal for quotes, orders, service history and e-invoices — making the "one-stop" promise visible and self-serve.
