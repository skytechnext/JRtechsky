Operations

Future-state design — swimlanes, service blueprint and the data model.

Having mapped the broken current state, this page designs the target. BPMN-style swimlane diagrams show who does what across the redesigned quote-to-cash and field-service flows; a service blueprint separates frontstage, backstage and support; and a UML class diagram defines the Odoo 19 data model the whole platform stands on.

1 · BPMN swimlane — future-state quote-to-cash

Each lane is a role; each step is a single Odoo action with a clear owner. No re-keying — the record moves, the people don't rebuild it.

3 · BPMN swimlane — chemical subscription resupply

The recurring engine: a subscription plan drives delivery, stock replenishment and invoicing on a cycle, with no customer phone call and no manual order — the antidote to today's stock-outs.
