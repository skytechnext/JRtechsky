Delivery

A phased Odoo 19 roadmap that banks value early
and de-risks the hard apps later.

JR-Tech's four business lines cannot all go live at once without overwhelming a ~40-person SME. We sequence the rollout into three phases over six to nine months — start with the financial and commercial backbone, layer in fabrication and field service once people trust the core, and finish with the recurring-revenue and AI capabilities that compound the return.

Phase detail

Phase 1 · Months 1–2

Commercial & financial backbone

Scope: stand up the single source of truth for money and stock. Every quote, invoice and stock movement across all four lines starts flowing through Odoo.

Modules: Accounting (SST + MyInvois e-invoicing), CRM, Sales, Inventory, Contacts.

Milestones:

  • Chart of accounts & SST tax config signed off
  • Customer/supplier master migrated & de-duplicated
  • First MyInvois-compliant e-invoice issued
  • Opening stock counted and loaded

Exit criteria: a full quote-to-cash cycle runs in production for one week with a balanced trial balance and <2% stock variance against the count.

Phase 2 · Months 3–5

Operations & service engine

Scope: bring JR-Tech's physical work — fabrication, equipment rental and on-site service — onto the platform so jobs, assets and engineers are visible end-to-end.

Modules: Manufacturing (stainless-steel make-to-order), Field Service, Rental, Maintenance, Repair.

Milestones:

  • Fabrication BoMs & work-order routings built
  • Rental asset register & contract templates live
  • Field engineers dispatching from mobile
  • Preventive-maintenance schedules generating tickets

Exit criteria: a fabrication job, a rental contract and a grease-trap service visit each complete in-system with costs landing back on the customer P&L.

Phase 3 · Months 6–9

Recurring revenue & intelligence

Scope: turn JR-Tech's chemical resupply into predictable subscription revenue, open a digital channel, and switch on the AI and analytics that pay back the investment.

Modules: Subscriptions, eCommerce/Website, AI (assisted quoting + predictive maintenance), Dashboards/BI.

Milestones:

  • Chemical subscription plans & auto-reorder live
  • Spare-parts & chemicals web store published
  • AI quote-draft & maintenance-risk models in use
  • Executive KPI dashboard adopted by leadership

Exit criteria: recurring invoices auto-generate, the BI dashboard reconciles to the GL, and AI suggestions are accepted on a majority of eligible quotes.

Effort & value profile by phase

Front-load discipline, back-load payoff

Phase 1 is deliberately the heaviest on TechNext consulting effort relative to the number of apps — getting the ledger, tax and master data right is non-negotiable and every later phase inherits it. Phase 3 carries the lightest configuration burden but the highest cumulative business value, because subscriptions and AI act on data the earlier phases already made trustworthy.

Sequencing rule: no app goes into a later phase if a Phase 1 app it depends on has not cleared its exit criteria. Manufacturing waits for clean Inventory; Subscriptions wait for clean Accounting.

How each phase is run

Every phase follows the same disciplined cadence, so JR-Tech's team learns one rhythm and repeats it three times with growing confidence.

  1. Discover & confirm. Re-validate process maps and the BRD for the phase's apps with the relevant super-users; freeze scope before any configuration.
  2. Configure & iterate. Build in a sandbox, demo working software fortnightly, and adjust against real JR-Tech scenarios rather than abstract requirements.
  3. Migrate & reconcile. Load and cleanse the data this phase needs, then reconcile against source records before sign-off.
  4. Test & accept. Run scripted UAT with super-users; defects are triaged and the phase is gated on the documented exit criteria — not on a calendar date.
  5. Train & go live. Role-based training immediately before cut-over, then a time-boxed hypercare window with on-call support.
  6. Stabilise & hand on. Close hypercare, capture lessons, and start discovery for the next phase already in flight.
