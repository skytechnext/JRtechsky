Benefit-realisation timeline

Value compounds; it does not switch on

Benefits accrue gradually as adoption matures and clean data accumulates. The curve shows cumulative realised benefit as a percentage of the steady-state target. There is a modest investment trough during implementation, an inflection at each go-live, and a steepening as Phase 3 subscriptions and AI compound on top of a trusted core.

Measurement discipline: benefit is only booked once the KPI it relates to has cleared its baseline measurement and is tracked in the live BI dashboard — not on go-live day.

Leading vs lagging indicators

We steer with leading indicators because they move first and can be acted on this week; we report lagging indicators because they are the outcomes leadership cares about. Watching only the lagging ones means finding out too late.

Leading — watched weekly

  • Quotes raised in Odoo vs outside it (adoption)
  • % service jobs scheduled in-system
  • Stock adjustments / cycle-count variance
  • Open support tickets & ageing
  • AI quote suggestions accepted (Phase 3)

These predict whether the lagging numbers will improve.

Lagging — reported to leadership

  • Quote turnaround time
  • On-time service completion rate
  • DSO and cash conversion
  • Gross margin by line / job
  • Recurring revenue from chemical subscriptions

These are the proof the investment paid back.
