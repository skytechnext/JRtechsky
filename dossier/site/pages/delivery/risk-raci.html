Delivery

Naming the risks early — and saying exactly who owns each one.

An ERP programme fails quietly when nobody owns a risk and nobody knows who decides. This page sets out a live risk register scored on probability and impact, plotted on a heatmap so the few risks that truly threaten the programme are unmistakable — paired with a project RACI matrix that fixes accountability across every workstream and role.

Risk heatmap

Each bubble is a register risk positioned by probability (x) and impact (y); bubble size and colour reflect the resulting exposure score. Anything in the upper-right carries an explicit, owned mitigation. Hover a point for the risk it represents.
