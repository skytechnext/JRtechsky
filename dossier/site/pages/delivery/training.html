Delivery

People do not adopt what they were never taught to use.

Delivery methods

Instructor-led

Hands-on workshops

Small-group sessions in a sandbox loaded with JR-Tech's own data — users do the task, they don't watch a slide. Best for power users.

Train-the-trainer

Super-user cascade

Super-users go deep, then teach their own teams. Peer-delivered training is faster to absorb and builds lasting in-house capability.

Just-in-time

Job aids & quick guides

One-page, role-specific cheat-sheets and short screen recordings at the point of need — living in the knowledge base built during hypercare.

On-site

Field ride-alongs

For engineers, training happens on a real job with a super-user beside them, not in a meeting room away from the work.

Sandbox

Safe practice environment

A non-production instance where users rehearse end-to-end flows and make mistakes with zero consequence before go-live.

Reinforcement

Hypercare floor-walking

In the first weeks, support is physically present, turning every real transaction into a coaching moment.

Competency ramp

From aware to expert, on a predictable curve

The chart tracks expected competency for a typical operator and a super-user across the learning journey. Super-users ramp faster and higher because they start a cycle earlier and train others. Operators reach working competency by go-live and self-sufficiency through hypercare.

Gate: a user must reach "working competency" — demonstrated on the sign-off checklist — before being granted live transactional access. Below the line, they stay in supervised practice.

Certification & sign-off

  1. Complete the path. User finishes the role-based modules for the phase in scope.
  2. Demonstrate the task. User performs the core end-to-end flow unaided in the sandbox while a super-user observes against a checklist.
  3. Sign-off. Super-user and functional consultant confirm competency; the user signs the acknowledgement.
  4. Grant access. Live transactional permissions are enabled only after sign-off — tying R9 (insufficient training) in the risk register directly to access control.
  5. Reinforce. Hypercare floor-walking and refreshers close any residual gaps; a short knowledge-base certificate marks completion.
So what for JR-Tech: sign-off is not bureaucracy — it is the mechanism that guarantees the adoption rates in the KPI plan and protects the data quality the whole business case depends on.
