System of record

SQL Account — ERP edition

What it is

A widely-adopted Malaysian accounting & inventory system (by eStream Software / "SQL"), LHDN-approved and SST- and MyInvois-ready. The ERP edition adds stock, sales, purchasing and multi-location on top of the ledger. It is JR-Tech's official book of record and the source of the sales/DO data feeding the driver incentive KPIs.

What JR-Tech uses it for

  • Sales documents, Delivery Orders (DO) & invoicing
  • Inventory / stock & purchasing
  • General ledger, AR/AP, statutory reporting
  • SST and MyInvois e-invoice submission
  • Exports (e.g. Print Sales Document Listing) that feed downstream spreadsheets & KPIs

Fit assessment

What SQL Account does well — and where it stops

Keep leaning on it for

  • Malaysian statutory compliance — SST, LHDN, MyInvois e-invoicing already working.
  • Core bookkeeping — GL, AR/AP, tax — mature and familiar to the finance team.
  • Stock & invoicing — item masters, DO and invoice documents.

Where it can't reach

  • No CRM / quoting pipeline — prospects & follow-ups live in memory and chat.
  • No manufacturing — stainless-steel fabrication has no BOMs or job costing.
  • No rental lifecycle — equipment on hire, returns and recurring billing are manual.
  • No field service — dishwasher/grease-trap dispatch & maintenance aren't scheduled.
  • No connected view — one order can't flow enquiry → build → deliver → invoice without re-typing.
Implication for the Odoo programme

Consolidate operations, protect compliance

The goal isn't to rip out a working compliance engine on day one — it's to end the re-keying and give every department one connected system.

Recommended path

  • Phase 1 — stand up Odoo for the operational gaps (CRM/quoting, fabrication, rental, field service) and migrate master data from SQL Account.
  • Phase 2 — move accounting into Odoo (with SST/MyInvois), run parallel with SQL Account for one cycle, reconcile to the cent, then retire the duplicate.
  • Alternative — if finance prefers, keep SQL Account as the statutory ledger and integrate it with Odoo via export/API until confidence is built.

Migration & continuity

  • Export customers, suppliers, item masters, price lists, opening balances and historical DO/invoices from SQL Account.
  • Load via Odoo's import wizard (interim) or a scripted API load — see the Data Migration plan.
  • Preserve MyInvois continuity — no gap in e-invoice submission across cutover (see Security, Compliance & MyInvois).
Guiding rule. Compliance never goes dark. SQL Account stays live until Odoo has passed a full-cycle reconciliation and a clean MyInvois submission in parallel.
