MyInvois — the LHDN e-Invoicing mandate

Malaysia's Inland Revenue Board (LHDN / IRBM) is rolling out mandatory e-Invoicing through the MyInvois platform in phases by annual turnover. Each invoice must be submitted for clearance, validated by LHDN, and returned with a unique identifier and a QR code before it is shared with the buyer.

PhaseTaxpayer threshold (annual turnover)Mandatory from
Phase 1> RM100 million1 Aug 2024
Phase 2> RM25m up to RM100m1 Jan 2025
Phase 3> RM5m up to RM25m1 Jul 2025
Phase 4> RM1m up to RM5m1 Jan 2026
Phase 5up to RM1m (with low-turnover exemptions/grace as announced)1 Jul 2026 (per latest LHDN schedule)
Where JR-Tech sits: as an established F&B engineering SME, JR-Tech almost certainly falls inside the 2025–2026 phases. The system must be e-invoicing-capable at go-live regardless of the exact phase date — LHDN has continued to adjust the lower-turnover timeline, so we build for compliance now, not later. Confirm the applicable phase against JR-Tech's audited turnover during discovery.

How Odoo 19 complies

sequenceDiagram participant U as JR-Tech (Odoo) participant L as LHDN MyInvois participant B as Customer U->>U: Confirm invoice (account.move) U->>L: Submit e-invoice (REST, signed) L-->>U: Validate → UIN + QR + status U->>B: Share validated invoice (PDF + QR) B-->>L: (Optional) reject within window L-->>U: Status updates / cancellation

The Odoo flow

  • Localisation connector — Odoo's Malaysian e-invoicing module maps invoice fields to the MyInvois schema and submits via the LHDN API.
  • Clearance model — invoices are transmitted for validation; the returned UIN and QR code are stored on the account.move and printed on the document.
  • Master data — buyer/supplier TIN, SST registration and classification codes are captured on res.partner (loaded during migration).
  • Consolidation & B2C — supported for receipts where individual e-invoices are not requested, per LHDN rules.
  • Status sync — validation/rejection/cancellation states are tracked back on the invoice for a full audit trail.

SST handling

Tax configuration

Odoo's MY localisation ships Sales & Service Tax codes. JR-Tech's mix spans taxable goods (equipment, chemicals) and taxable services (maintenance, grease-trap, repair) — each line carries the correct SST treatment, computed automatically and posted to the right tax accounts.

Reporting

Tax is summarised in Odoo's tax reports for the SST return, with the audit trail from each invoice line back to the journal entry. SST amounts also flow into the MyInvois submission, keeping the e-invoice and the tax return consistent from one source.

Data protection — PDPA

PDPA

Lawful processing

Customer and employee personal data is processed for defined business purposes with consent captured where required; data-subject access/correction is supported through Odoo records.

Minimise

Data minimisation

Only data needed for the transaction is stored; sensitive fields are restricted by role, and retention follows a defined schedule.

Secure

Security safeguards

Encryption in transit (TLS), encrypted backups, hosting-level access controls, and data residency confirmed via the hosting choice (Odoo.sh regional / on-prem).

Access control & roles

Odoo's security model combines groups (role membership), access rights (model-level CRUD) and record rules (row-level filters). Roles are scoped to JR-Tech's four lines so staff see only what they should.

Controls: enforce two-factor authentication for admin and finance, segregation of duties (the person who creates a vendor bill is not the one who pays it), and periodic access reviews.

Backups, DR & audit trail

Backups

Automated & tested

On Odoo.sh, daily backups with point-in-time restore are managed by the platform. Restores are tested periodically — an untested backup is not a backup.

DR

Recovery objectives

Target RPO ≤ 24h and RTO measured in hours via platform restore. A documented runbook names who does what during an incident.

Audit

Immutable trail

Odoo's chatter and audit logging track who changed what and when; posted journal entries are locked, and e-invoice status history is retained for LHDN.

So what for the Odoo build: compliance is not a bolt-on. The MyInvois connector, SST tax codes, PDPA-aware roles and the backup/DR posture are configured in Phase 1 alongside core finance on the roadmap — so JR-Tech is audit- and e-invoicing-ready from the first live invoice.
