The module map

Odoo is a modular ERP on a single shared data model — partners, products, stock moves and journal entries are reused by every app, which is what eliminates the re-keying JR-Tech does today. Modules below are grouped by domain; all post to one Accounting ledger.

Front office

Sell & engage

  • CRM — pipeline, predictive lead scoring
  • Sales — quotations, order management
  • Subscriptions — recurring chemical resupply
  • Website / eCommerce — catalogue & web RFQs
  • POS — counter / showroom sales
  • Helpdesk — customer tickets & SLAs
Supply & make

Stock, buy, fabricate

  • Inventory — multi-warehouse, lots/serials, barcode
  • Purchase — RFQs, vendor bills, reordering
  • Manufacturing (MRP) — stainless-steel BoMs & work orders
  • Quality — QC checks on MO & receipts
  • Barcode — warehouse & fabrication scanning
Service & assets

Finance & compliance

  • Sign — e-signature on contracts & service reports

Edition recommendation — Community vs Enterprise

Odoo Community (free, OSS)

What it covers

CRM, Sales, Inventory, Purchase, Manufacturing, Project, Website and Accounting basics. Strong foundation, but several apps JR-Tech depends on are Enterprise-only.

Gaps for JR-Tech: no Field Service, no full Subscriptions, no Documents OCR / Invoice Digitisation, no Sign, no Studio, no Helpdesk, no Quality, no Odoo 19 AI text, and only a partial Accounting (no advanced reconciliation, follow-ups, or the localised MyInvois e-invoicing tooling).

Odoo Enterprise — recommended

Why JR-Tech needs it

  • Field Service, Subscriptions, Helpdesk, Quality, Repair — core to the service and chemical-supply lines.
  • Documents + OCR and Sign — the AP automation and paperless service reports in the AI catalog.
  • Studio — low-code fit of JR-Tech's bespoke flows without forking code.
  • Full Accounting + MY localisation — SST and MyInvois compliance.
  • Odoo 19 AI (AI text, predictive scoring) — the native quick wins.
Recommendation: deploy Odoo 19 Enterprise. The AI/OCR/Field-Service/Subscriptions capabilities that the JR-Tech business case rests on are Enterprise-only; replicating them on Community via third-party OCA modules would cost more in integration and maintenance than the per-user Enterprise licence, and forfeit the supported MyInvois localisation. Per-user pricing is confirmed at contracting in the ROI / TCO Model.

Recommendation: host on Odoo.sh. JR-Tech does not run a datacentre or a dedicated infra team; Odoo.sh gives managed backups, staging branches for safe upgrades, and Git-based deployment of any Studio/custom modules — removing infrastructure risk from a small business. Odoo 19 native AI features

AI text & assistant

In-context generation across Sales, CRM, Documents and email — drafting quotes, replies and document narratives grounded on the record. Powers use-cases 1, 6 and 7 in the AI catalog.

Invoice / document digitisation (OCR)

Reads vendor bills, receipts and expenses into draft accounting entries with line matching — the AP-automation quick win.

Predictive lead scoring

Built-in CRM probability model ranks leads on historical conversion patterns once sufficient pipeline data exists.

So what: the architecture is deliberately native-first. Three of JR-Tech's earliest AI wins need no custom model — they are configuration on Enterprise. Bespoke models (demand forecasting, vision QC, predictive maintenance) attach to this same data model later via the connectors on the Integration & API Map.
