03 · The uncomfortable truth

Why another ERP team won't be good for JR-Tech

Here's how a typical ERP sale works — and why it hurts companies like yours.

The salesperson sitting across from you is paid on deal size. So they design for complexity: "the more items, modules and codes I can put in, the higher my sales KPI." Every extra module is commission for them — and paperwork for your staff. Six months later, your team is fighting the system: more screens, more codes, more steps to do the same job they used to do on paper. The famous names are the worst offenders — SAP is known for being slow and difficult to use, built for corporate armies with IT departments, not for a Penang SME where the boss still walks the workshop floor.

The typical ERP vendor designs for THEIR KPI

  • Paid on deal size → sells you complexity
  • More modules, more codes, more screens = bigger invoice
  • Your staff inherit the paperwork and the headaches
  • Slow, rigid systems (SAP's reputation exists for a reason)
  • After go-live, the salesperson disappears — support becomes a ticket queue

SKY designs for YOUR P&L

  • Fewer clicks, fewer codes — simplicity is the KPI
  • Only the apps JR-Tech actually needs, phased in
  • AI and automations remove paperwork instead of adding it
  • Fast platform your drivers and welders will actually use
  • I stay accountable — my name and my other Penang clients are on this
Don't take my word on speed — Odoo's own head-to-head with SAP.
