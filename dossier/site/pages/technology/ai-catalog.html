How to read this catalog

Each use-case is a card: the business trigger that fires it, the Odoo/AI mechanism that delivers it, the build effort (S/M/L) and the expected impact. Native capabilities (Odoo 19 AI text, OCR digitisation, forecasting) are labelled — those carry the least delivery risk and are sequenced first. Custom AI services (vision QC, demand forecasting on chemicals) are framed honestly as later-phase bets.

Sequencing principle: ship the four native quick wins in Phase 2 of the roadmap to bank ROI and build trust, then invest the savings into the data-hungry custom models in Phase 3.

The nine use-cases

Native-assisted

1 · AI-assisted quoting

Generates first-draft quotation lines, scope notes and cover text from a short brief across all four business lines.

TriggerNew CRM opportunity / RFQ received via web or WhatsApp
MechanismOdoo 19 AI text on Sales + product/price templates; historical won-quote retrieval
EffortS config + prompt library
ImpactHigh quote turnaround 2 days → hours
Native-assisted

2 · Lead scoring

Ranks inbound enquiries so the small sales team works the hottest HORECA leads first instead of first-come-first-served.

TriggerLead created / updated in CRM
MechanismOdoo CRM predictive lead scoring (probability model on historical conversions)
EffortS built-in, needs ~6 mo data
ImpactMed higher win-rate on finite sales capacity
Custom model

3 · Demand forecasting — chemicals

Predicts dishwasher / laundry / pool chemical reorder volumes per site so resupply runs become proactive subscriptions, not reactive calls.

TriggerDaily batch on consumption & delivery history
MechanismTime-series forecast feeding Inventory reordering rules + Subscriptions
EffortM external model + connector
ImpactHigh stable recurring revenue, fewer stock-outs
Custom model

4 · Predictive maintenance

Flags rental dishwashers and kitchen equipment likely to fail, converting emergency callouts into planned, profitable service visits.

TriggerTelemetry / service-history thresholds, runtime hours
MechanismOdoo Maintenance preventive plans + risk score from telemetry (see Integration Map)
EffortL needs IoT data maturity
ImpactHigh uptime SLA + maintenance margin
Native (OCR)

5 · OCR / AP automation

Reads supplier invoices and receipts into draft vendor bills — killing the manual keying that delays month-end close.

TriggerSupplier bill emailed / uploaded to Documents
MechanismOdoo Invoice Digitization (OCR) → draft account.move with line matching
EffortS native, per-page credits
ImpactHigh AP keying ~80% eliminated
Native-assisted

6 · Customer / WhatsApp chatbot

Answers product, rental-availability and service-status questions, captures leads and books service slots after hours.

TriggerInbound chat on website / WhatsApp Business
MechanismOdoo Livechat + AI replies grounded on product & Helpdesk knowledge base
EffortM WhatsApp connector + KB curation
ImpactMed faster response, lead capture 24/7
Native-assisted

7 · Document generation

Drafts service reports, maintenance summaries, method statements and proposal narratives from structured Odoo records.

TriggerService order closed / proposal requested
MechanismOdoo 19 AI text on document fields + Sign for execution
EffortS prompt + template config
ImpactMed consistent, faster paperwork
Custom model

8 · Vision QC — fabrication

Inspects in-house stainless-steel fabrication (welds, dimensions, finish) via camera to standardise quality before delivery.

TriggerFabrication work-order QC checkpoint
MechanismVision model → Odoo Quality check result on the MO; defects raise alerts
EffortL bespoke, training images
ImpactMed rework & warranty cost down
Automation

9 · Smart reorder & resupply

Closes the loop: forecasts feed reorder rules and auto-generate purchase orders and chemical resupply subscription renewals.

TriggerStock below min / subscription cycle
MechanismInventory reordering rules + Purchase RFQ automation + Subscriptions
EffortS native rules engine
ImpactMed fewer stock-outs, less working capital

Impact vs. effort

Bubble size = relative confidence/data-readiness today (larger = more ready). The top-left "do-first" quadrant — high impact, low effort — is dominated by native Odoo 19 capabilities. The high-effort bets sit lower-right and depend on data maturity that only accrues once the ERP is live.
