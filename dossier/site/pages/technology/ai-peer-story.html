AI in Action — A Peer Story

What happens when a kitchen-equipment SME just like JR-Tech puts its business on a unified ERP with AI assistance? This is the journey of "Selatan Kitchen Engineering" — a composite peer — from spreadsheet chaos to a measurable operational turnaround.

Illustrative composite. "Selatan Kitchen Engineering" is not a real company. It is a composite peer assembled by TechNext from anonymised, typical outcomes of Malaysian HORECA / kitchen-equipment SMEs that adopted Odoo + AI. The metrics are modelled to be realistic and conservative for a business of JR-Tech's size — see Methodology & Assumptions. Treat them as a plausible target trajectory, not a guarantee.

Selatan sold and rented commercial kitchen equipment, ran a fabrication workshop, dispatched a service crew for installs and grease-trap work, and resupplied cleaning chemicals — the same shape as JR-Tech. Before the project, it ran on a tangle of Excel quote sheets, a WhatsApp group per customer, and a bookkeeper closing the month three weeks late.

Before & after — the operating picture

Before

Disconnected, reactive, invisible

  • Quotes hand-built in Excel; no version control, ~2-day turnaround.
  • Rental assets tracked on a whiteboard — double-bookings and "lost" units.
  • Service calls triaged over WhatsApp; no SLA, no history.
  • Chemical resupply was reactive — customers ran out, then called.
  • Vendor bills keyed by hand; month-end close +21 days.
  • No view of margin by line until the accountant reconciled quarterly.
After (Odoo 19 + AI)

Unified, proactive, measured

  • AI-assisted quotes drafted in minutes from a brief; one source of truth.
  • Rental lifecycle in Odoo — utilisation visible, no double-booking.
  • Field Service with scheduled SLAs and full equipment service history.
  • Chemical resupply on auto-renewing subscriptions with forecasted volumes.
  • OCR turns supplier bills into draft entries; close in ~5 days.
  • Live margin-by-line dashboard for the owner, daily.

Lessons that transfer to JR-Tech

Lesson 1

Clean data first, AI second

The forecasting and lead-scoring wins only appeared once 6+ months of clean transactional data existed. Selatan's delay was data, not models — see Data Migration.

Lesson 2

Quote speed is the gateway win

AI quoting was the change staff felt first and championed. It bought goodwill for the tougher field-service discipline that followed.

Lesson 3

Recurring revenue is the prize

Turning ad-hoc chemical orders into forecasted subscriptions was the single biggest margin and valuation lever — bigger than any efficiency cut.

Lesson 4

Field staff need mobile, not desktop

Adoption stalled until the service crew got the Odoo mobile + barcode flow. Plan enablement around the van, not the office.

Lesson 5

One owner-sponsor, weekly

The director's weekly review of the live dashboard drove discipline. AI surfaces the signal; leadership has to act on it.

Lesson 6

Start native, customise sparingly

Native Odoo AI/OCR delivered ~70% of the value at a fraction of the cost; bespoke vision QC stayed a deliberate, later bet.
