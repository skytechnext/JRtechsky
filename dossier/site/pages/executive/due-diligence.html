1 · Corporate

Entity & governance

Legal name: JR-Tech Solution Sdn Bhd · SSM Reg.: 200901013633 (incorporated ~2009). Director: Tan Gim Boon. Registered office: 556-Y, Mukim 13, Batu Uban, Jalan Sultan Azlan Shah, 11700 Gelugor, Penang.

A private limited company with ~16 years of continuous trading signals stability and an established reputation in the Penang F&B engineering market.

Watch-items

  • Key-person concentration around the director — succession & delegation depth to confirm.
  • Shareholding structure & any related-party suppliers to verify in formal DD.
  • Statutory filings (annual returns, audited accounts) to be requested.
So what for the build: a single director/sponsor simplifies decision-making for the Odoo rollout but raises change-management dependency — addressed in Change Management.

3 · Operational

JR-Tech's operational capability is genuine — in-house fabrication, regulatory kitchen design, after-sales service. The weakness is the connective tissue between functions.

5 · Technology

This is the lowest-scoring lens and the reason for the engagement. The business runs on general-purpose tools with no system of record.

Gap

No ERP

Spreadsheets and WhatsApp act as the de-facto operating system across all four lines.

Gap

No single customer record

The same HORECA client may appear differently in quoting, service and chemical resupply.

Risk

Data fragility

Business-critical data on personal devices and local files — backup & continuity risk.

So what for the build: a clean slate is an advantage — no legacy ERP to decommission. Migration is data clean-up + load, not a system rip-out. See Data Migration.

As an F&B kitchen engineering and chemical-supply business, JR-Tech sits under several Malaysian regulatory regimes. Most are well within reach; two need confirmation before go-live.

Overall DD verdict

JR-Tech is a sound, long-established business with a clear digital deficit rather than a structural problem. No red flags sit in corporate or commercial fundamentals; both reds are addressable in Phase 1. The risk profile is favourable for an ERP transformation.

Recommendation: proceed. Close the two red items in Phase 1, work the ambers across Phases 2–3. Continue to the Executive Summary for the plan and ask.
