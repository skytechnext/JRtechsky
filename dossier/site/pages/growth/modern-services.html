Growth & Strategy

New service lines that turn JR-Tech's install base into recurring, AI-enabled revenue.

JR-Tech already touches the kitchen at every point — equipment, fabrication, service and chemicals. The next move is to monetise that footprint as recurring, outcome-based services that ride the brand's own "Experience the Future of Kitchens" positioning. This page proposes five modern service lines, sizes the opportunity, and shows how each one is unlocked by the Odoo 19 platform.

Five new service lines

EaaS

Equipment-as-a-Service

Subscription access to ovens, dishwashers and induction ranges — bundled install, servicing and upgrade path — instead of capex. Extends the existing rental line into a true managed offer.

PdM

Predictive-maintenance subscription

IoT sensors on critical equipment flag faults before failure; sold as an uptime subscription on top of the Gold service tier.

Audit

Energy-efficiency audits

Paid kitchen energy & sustainability assessments — directly on-brand with the energy-efficiency / smokeless / cook-&-chill positioning — feeding equipment & retrofit sales.

KaaS

Kitchen-as-a-Service / cloud-kitchen fit-out

Turnkey design-build-operate packages for cloud-kitchen and central-kitchen operators — JR-Tech's consulting + fabrication + equipment + chemicals as one subscription-style deal.

IoT

Chemical auto-replenishment IoT

Smart dosing units sense consumption and auto-reorder warewash, laundry and pool chemicals — zero-touch resupply with the highest switching cost of all.

∑

One platform underneath

Every line above is a recurring contract, an asset to track and a service to dispatch — exactly what Odoo 19 already manages for the core business.

Opportunity sizing

Where to lean first

Plotted as 3-year revenue potential against time-to-launch. The fastest, stickiest wins extend things JR-Tech already does:

  • Chemical auto-replenish IoT — quickest to launch, defends the chemical line.
  • Predictive maintenance — natural up-sell on the service base.
  • EaaS & KaaS — largest prize, longer build, capital-aware.
  • Energy audits — low-cost lead engine that pulls equipment sales.
So-what: these are not greenfield ventures — they are recurring-revenue wrappers around the existing four lines.

Recurring-revenue (ARR) build-up — 3 years

Modelled ARR from the new recurring lines, stacked over three years. This is additive to the ~RM18m core revenue base and consistent with the FY28 ~RM27.8m trajectory — the recurring layer is the highest-quality slice of that growth.

By end of Year 3 the four recurring lines compound to roughly RM2.9m ARR — predictable, high-margin revenue that de-risks the lumpier project and fabrication income and helps fund the larger EaaS/KaaS asset bets.
