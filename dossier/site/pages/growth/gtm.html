A digital go-to-market engine that fills the funnel and nurtures it inside Odoo.

JR-Tech's pipeline today is relationship- and referral-led — durable but unscalable, and invisible until a deal lands. This page defines the ideal customer profile, the channel mix, a content and SEO plan, the lead funnel, and a CRM-driven nurture and marketing-automation model that runs natively in Odoo 19 — turning a quiet brand into a measurable demand engine.

Channel mix

Inbound & SEO

Rank for high-intent terms — "commercial kitchen fabrication Malaysia", "warewash chemical supplier Penang", "kitchen equipment rental". Pillar pages per line + local SEO for each branch region.

Account-based outbound

Targeted outreach to hotel groups and chains at refurb/expansion triggers; consultant & contractor partnerships for project flow.

Referral & partner

Formalise the existing referral strength into a tracked partner programme with consultants, F&B associations and architects.

Content & thought leadership

Energy-efficiency, smokeless & cook-&-chill explainers; compliance guides; case studies — the "Future of Kitchens" narrative as demand content.

Paid & social

LinkedIn for B2B decision-makers; Google Search for high-intent; retargeting on long fit-out consideration cycles.

Events & demo

Trade shows and showroom demos of AI-induction / smokeless tech to anchor the premium, on-brand positioning.

Lead funnel

From visitor to recurring account

The funnel makes the demand engine measurable for the first time. Illustrative monthly volumes show conversion from traffic to closed-won, with the recurring lines (chemicals, service) extending lifetime value well past the first deal.

  • Visitors → leads via gated tools & content.
  • Leads → MQLs scored on ICP fit & intent.
  • MQLs → opportunities handed to sales in Odoo CRM.
  • Opportunities → closed-won, then onboarded to recurring contracts.
