The problem

Running a commercial kitchen is hard enough without juggling five vendors.

Most operators stitch together a designer, a fabricator, an equipment dealer, a repair contractor and a chemical supplier — and inherit every gap between them. The gaps cost you time, money and inspections.

Downtime that stops service

A failed dishwasher or chiller can shut a line in minutes. Slow callouts mean lost covers and spoiled stock.

Compliance pressure

Local authority, hygiene and fire-safety requirements are unforgiving — and rework after an inspection is expensive.

Layouts that fight the work

Poor space planning slows every shift, wastes energy and makes cleaning a chore staff quietly skip.

Too many vendors

Five suppliers, five invoices, five people to chase when something breaks — and nobody owns the whole kitchen.

The JR-Tech way

One accountable partner, from blueprint to daily operations.

We carry your kitchen through every stage — and stay on after handover. Because we fabricate in-house and supply the equipment and chemicals ourselves, the responsibility never falls between the cracks.

About JR-Tech

A Penang workshop that grew into a one-stop kitchen partner.

JR-Tech Solution Sdn Bhd has served Malaysia's foodservice industry since 2009. What began with equipment and fabrication grew into three coordinated business lines — consulting, equipment and chemicals — so operators could finally deal with one accountable partner instead of five.

From our base in Gelugor, Penang, we design, build, equip and maintain commercial kitchens for hotels, restaurants, institutions and industrial caterers across the country.
