Know

Ideal client profile

Best fit: a real product or service that buyers already love, hidden behind a weak digital presence.

  • Segment — B2B SME — F&B, commercial kitchen, industrial, trades or professional services — selling considered purchases.
  • Symptom — pipeline depends on referrals and the owner's network; quiet months cause panic.
  • Digital state — dated or broken website, little/no SEO, no content, no lead capture, no analytics.
  • Trigger — a growth target, a new competitor winning online, a failed DIY attempt, or a rebrand.
  • Authority & budget — owner/GM cares about leads and revenue, not "likes," and can fund a retainer.
Know

Qualify & disqualify fast

Lean in 🟢

  • Wants measurable leads, not awareness fluff
  • Product/service quality is genuinely strong
  • Accepts marketing as an investment with a ramp
  • Willing to commit 6–12 months

Walk away 🔴

  • Wants "viral" / overnight results
  • One-off cheap website, nothing more
  • Won't share data or sales numbers
  • Product/market fit isn't there yet
Run

Discovery questions

Anchor on the funnel and the money. Make the referral ceiling visible.

Where leads come from today
If I asked where your last 10 customers came from, what would you say? What happens in a month the referrals dry up?
The digital front door
When a buyer Googles what you sell, what do they find — you, or a competitor? When did you last update the site?
Goals & economics
What's a new customer worth to you? How many more a month would change the business? What's the cost of one slow quarter?
History & decision
Tried marketing before — what happened? Who decides on this, and what would make it a "now" priority?
Run

Objection handling

They sayYou say
"We get all our work by word of mouth."That's a strength — and a ceiling. Referrals are people who already found you; marketing reaches the larger group searching right now and finding a competitor.
"Marketing doesn't work for our industry."Your buyers research online before they call — every B2B buyer does. The question isn't if they search, it's whether you show up.
"We tried an agency and got nothing."Most agencies sell activity, not pipeline. We wire everything to your CRM and report on leads and revenue. If a channel doesn't convert, we cut it.
"We'll just do Facebook ourselves."Great for staying in touch with existing fans; weak for capturing new demand. We make the whole funnel work, not one channel.
"SEO takes too long."True — that's why we pair the slow-compounding engine (SEO/content) with fast channels (a converting site + targeted ads) so you see movement early.

Close

The call plan

  • Before — run a 10-minute audit of their site & search; screenshot one competitor outranking them.
  • Open — frame the referral ceiling, then discovery. Let them feel the gap.
  • Show — the competitor comparison, the site we built, and the marketing plan as method.
  • Close — offer the growth audit as the no-risk next step; book it before you leave.
  • After — same-day recap with the three quick wins you spotted and a link to the presentation.
