The problem

Growth is hiding the cracks

Every new order adds another spreadsheet, another chat thread, another re-keyed number. The team works harder; the owner sees less.

🧾

Quotes that crawl

A wok-burner enquiry bounces between sales, fabrication and the boss for days. Versions diverge. Margins are a guess.

📦

Blind inventory & rentals

Which dishwasher is on rent, where, and due back when? Chemicals reordered late or over-stocked. No single answer.

⚖️

Compliance pressure

Malaysia's e-invoicing (MyInvois) and SST aren't optional. Manual workarounds don't scale and don't pass an audit.

The solution

One connected platform on Odoo 19

Every department works in the same system, on the same data, in real time — with AI assists where they pay off.

  • Sales & CRM — AI-assisted quoting from a product & fabrication catalog, e-signature, pipeline visibility.
  • Manufacturing — stainless-steel fabrication with BOMs, work orders and real job costing.
  • Rental — equipment lifecycle, availability, contracts and recurring billing in one register.
  • Field service — dispatch, maintenance schedules, grease-trap & service jobs with mobile sign-off.
  • Inventory & purchasing — chemical and parts stock with reordering rules and supplier orders.
  • Finance — SST, MyInvois e-invoicing, multi-currency and live P&L — no re-keying.
