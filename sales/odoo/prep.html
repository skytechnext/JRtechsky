Know

Ideal client profile

We're a strong fit when most of these are true. The closer the match, the shorter the sales cycle.

  • Segment — Malaysian F&B / commercial-kitchen business: fabrication, equipment sales & rental, maintenance, and/or cleaning chemicals.
  • Size — roughly 10–100 staff, multi-line operations, growth-stage and feeling the seams.
  • Symptoms — quotes in chat, stock in spreadsheets, paper job-sheets, accounts re-keyed, no single source of truth.
  • Trigger — MyInvois / SST pressure, a botched audit, a missed rental return, or an owner who "can't see the numbers."
  • Authority — owner-operator or GM involved; they feel the pain personally.
Know

Qualify & disqualify fast

Lean in 🟢

  • Multiple disconnected tools today
  • Owner wants real-time margin / cash visibility
  • Compliance deadline creating urgency
  • Budget conversation isn't taboo

Walk away 🔴

  • Shopping purely on lowest licence price
  • No internal owner for the project
  • Wants a custom build, not a configured platform
  • "Just send a proposal" with no discovery
Run

Discovery questions

Open with process, not product. Let them describe the pain in their words.

Current state
Walk me through a quote from enquiry to invoice — which tools, who touches it, how long? Where does a number get typed twice?
The cost of the gap
When did you last lose a job to slow quoting, or eat margin you didn't see coming? How do you know which rentals are out right now?
Compliance
How are you handling SST and MyInvois today? What happens at audit time?
Decision & timing
Who else weighs in on a system like this? What would make this a "this year" project versus "someday"?
Run

Objection handling

They sayYou say
"Odoo looks expensive."Price the status quo: hours re-keying, lost jobs, audit risk. We deliver in phases so value lands before the full spend — and the licence is the small part.
"We'll just keep using Excel."Excel scales until it doesn't. The day a rental goes missing or an audit lands, the spreadsheet costs more than the system. We migrate your sheets, not bin them.
"Implementations always overrun."That's why we start with fixed-scope discovery and a phased go-live with rollback — not a big-bang. You see a runnable demo before cutover.
"My staff won't adopt it."Role-based training, staff guides and weeks of hypercare. We configure to their workflow, so it's less typing than today, not more.
"Can't we just buy Odoo and DIY?"You can — and most stall on MyInvois, costing and migration. We get you to value faster and stay for hypercare.

The call plan

  • Before — review their website & product lines; pick one workflow to map live; open the dossier tabs you'll show.
  • Open — 2-min framing, then discovery questions. Listen 70%.
  • Show — mirror their worst workflow in Odoo; pull up the ROI/TCO and roadmap from the dossier.
  • Close — propose the fixed-scope discovery as the next concrete step; agree a date before you leave.
  • After — recap email same day with the discovery scope and a link to the presentation.
