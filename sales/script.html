Ground rules

How we use WhatsApp with owners

Malaysian F&B owners live on WhatsApp — it is where the deal actually moves. Treat it like a handshake, not a mailing list.

  • One message, one ask. A greeting, the point, the link, the question. Never five bubbles in a row.
  • Link + three-line summary. Nobody opens a 20-page PDF on a phone. Say what is in it and what you want them to do.
  • Office hours only (9am–7pm, Mon–Sat) unless they message first. Owners work nights; we do not chase at night.
  • Match their register. If they write "boss", you can be warm; if they write full sentences, so do you. Emoji: one, at most two.
  • Three chases, then a call. After the third unanswered message, pick up the phone. The fourth message is a break-up, not a nudge.
  • Voice notes only if they do. Mirror the medium; never send a two-minute voice note to someone who types.
  • Every commercial message points to a record. Proposal, BRD, e-sign and invoice links come from Odoo, so the paper trail is the system's, not the chat's.
  • Confirm in writing what was agreed by voice. After every call, the recap message is the contract of intent.
  • Money messages are always polite and always specific. Invoice number, amount, due date, link — and a way out (call me).
  • Log it. Paste the sent message into the CRM lead's chatter, or use the WhatsApp integration. If it is not in Odoo, it did not happen.

Stage 01

Prospect — first contact

Goal: earn a 15-minute conversation. Not a sale, not a demo — a conversation about their workflow.

Cold call — 30 seconds

You"Hi {{client}}, {{rep}} from TechNext — I'll be quick, I know you're mid-service. We work with kitchen-equipment and F&B businesses in Penang. Since MyInvois became mandatory, most of the owners I speak to are re-keying every quote into their accounting system by hand.Most of the owners I speak to are winning on referrals and losing on Google — buyers search, and find a competitor. Is that true for you, or have you got it sorted?"

If "sorted""Good to hear — what are you using? … If it ever creaks, I'm one message away. Can I send you a one-pager so you have my number?"

If "true""Then it's worth 15 minutes. I'll map that one workflow with you and show you what it looks like in Odoowhen the funnel is wired up. If it isn't obviously better, I'll tell you. {{meeting}}, or is another day easier?"

If "send me something""Happy to. I'll WhatsApp the one-pager now — and I'll message on Thursday to see what you made of it. Fair?"

Referral intro — on the phone

You"Hi {{client}}, {{rep}} from TechNext. {{referrer}} suggested I call — we did the Odoo implementation planthe marketing site and plan for JR-Tech and they thought it might be relevant to {{company}}. Did they mention it?"

Then"What {{referrer}} saw was one thing: a quote that becomes a job that becomes an e-invoice without anyone re-typing it.a website that turns a search into a quote request and logs it to the CRM with its source. Would 15 minutes to look at your version of that be useful?"

Close"Great — {{meeting}} works for me. I'll send a confirmation on WhatsApp with what to have on hand. Thank you, {{client}}."

Stage 02

Discover — book it, run it, recap it

Goal: leave with the pain in their words, a number attached to it, and a date for the next step. Listen 70%.

Discovery call — 30 minutes

Open (2 min)"Thanks for the time, {{client}}. Here's how I'd like to use it: 20 minutes on how {{company}} works today — I'll ask questions and mostly listen — then 10 minutes where I show you your worst workflow running in Odoowhere you sit against three competitors online. At the end I'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit. Sound okay?"

Current state"Walk me through a quote — from the customer's first message to the invoice. Which tools does it touch? Who touches it? How long does it take? … Where does a number get typed twice?""If I asked where your last ten customers came from, what would you say? … When a buyer Googles what you sell, what do they find — you, or someone else? When was the site last updated?"

Cost of the gap"When did you last lose a job to slow quoting, or find margin gone at month-end? … Right now, which rentals are out, and when are they due back — could you tell me without calling someone?""What's a new customer worth to you over a year? How many more a month would change the business? … What did last quiet quarter cost?"

Compliance / history"How are SST and MyInvois handled today? What happens at audit time?""Have you tried marketing before — an agency, a freelancer, Facebook yourselves? What happened?"

Decision & timing"Who else weighs in on something like this? … What would make it a this-quarter project rather than a someday one?"

Show (10 min)Mirror the workflow they just described in Odoo: enquiry → quote → work order → e-invoice. Then open the dossier's ROI / TCO model with their numbers.Screen-share the search result for their core term with a competitor above them; then the JR-Tech marketing site and the plan as method.

Close"So — I heard {{pain}}, and it's costing you roughly [their number]. The next concrete step is a fixed-scope discovery: one to two weeks, we map your real workflows and you get a requirements blueprint and a fixed quote — credited back if you go ahead.growth audit: we review your site, search and three competitors and hand you the three highest-leverage moves — yours to keep either way. Shall I send the proposal for that today, and we walk through it {{meeting}}?"

If they hesitate"What would need to be true for this to be a yes? … If it's [X], let me address that now rather than in a proposal."

Stage 03

Propose — send, walk through, follow up, close

Goal: never let a proposal be read alone. Sell payback, not price. Every follow-up adds value or asks a real question.

Proposal walk-through — 20 minutes

Open"Before the numbers — did I capture the problem right? Page 1 says {{pain}}. Anything to add?"

Structure"Three parts: what we'll do, in what order, and what it costs against what the problem costs. We go in phases — sales and quoting first, because that's where your cash starts; fabrication, rental and service next; finance and MyInvois last so nothing breaks at month-end. You approve each phase before the next starts.We fix the foundation first — a site that converts, tracking, lead capture into your CRM — then we generate demand with SEO, content and targeted campaigns, and we report against leads and revenue every month."

Investment"The number is RM {{amount}} before SST. Against that: [their cost of the gap] a year. Payback is [n] months on the ROI model — here it is with your numbers, not ours."

Close"On a scale of one to ten, how close is this to something you'd sign? … What would get it to a ten?"

If 7–9"So it's [the gap]. If we solve that, we go ahead? … Good — I'll revise today and send the agreement for e-signature with it."

If <7"Thank you for being straight. What's the main thing? … Let me take that away rather than argue it — I'll come back with a proper answer by {{due}}."

Objections — the spoken versions

"Too expensive""Compared with what, {{client}}? If it's compared with zero, agreed. If it's compared with {{pain}} for another year, the model says otherwise — let me show you the line." "And you only commit to the first phase.""And the audit is yours to keep even if you stop there."

"Need to think about it""Of course. What specifically will you be weighing up? … Then let's put that on the table now — I'd rather you decide with the answer than without it."

"Partner must agree""Absolutely — they'll live with it too. Shall I run the same 20 minutes for both of you {{meeting}}, so you don't have to re-explain it?"

"We tried before and it failed""What went wrong? … That's exactly why we start with a fixed-scope discovery and a runnable demo before anyone cuts over.That's why we report on leads and revenue, not activity, and cut any channel that doesn't convert."

"Send me a cheaper version""I can phase it differently, yes — but I won't take out the parts that make it work. Which outcome matters least to you this year? We'll move that one to phase two."

Stage 04

Agree — BRDplan & scope sign-off and e-signature

Goal: a signed scope and a signed agreement, both in Odoo. Changes after sign-off are change requests — say so kindly and early.

BRDPlan walk-through & sign-off ask

Open"This document is the contract for what 'done' means. I'd rather spend an hour on it now than a month arguing later. We'll go section by section and I'll mark changes live."

Per section"Stakeholders — anyone missing? … Scope — anything here you don't need, or anything you assumed was in and isn't? … Acceptance criteria — if we deliver exactly this, are you happy?"

Sign-off"With those changes in, can you approve this version by {{due}}? A reply saying 'Approved' in WhatsApp is enough, and I'll attach it to the record. From that point, new requirements go through a short change request so the price and timeline stay honest."

E-signature — on the phone together

You"I've sent the Odoo Sign link to this chat. Open it on your phone — it's the same proposal we walked through, with the agreement in front. Scroll down; the yellow fields are yours."

Guide"Tap the first one — choose Draw or Type your name — Adopt. Now the initials on each page … and the date field fills itself. When every field is done, tap Complete at the top."

Confirm"You'll get the signed PDF by email in a minute, and so will I. Thank you, {{client}} — welcome aboard. The deposit e-invoice follows today, and kickoff is {{meeting}}."

If it asks to log in"Close it and tap the link from the chat again — no account is needed, that screen only appears if a browser tab was already logged in somewhere."

Stage 05

Invoice & collect

Goal: get paid on time without spending the relationship. Every invoice message has number, amount, due date, link — and a human way out.

Collections call — relationship first

Open"{{client}}, {{rep}} here — not a sales call. I'm calling about invoice {{invoice_no}}, which is [n] days past due, and I'd rather talk than keep messaging."

Listen"Is there a problem with the invoice itself, or is it cash timing? … Okay — thank you for telling me."

If cash timing"Let's not let it drag. Can you do half by {{due}} and the balance thirty days later? I'll re-issue the e-invoices to match, so your books stay clean."

If a dispute"Tell me exactly what doesn't match — I'll fix the invoice today if we got it wrong, and if the work is the issue, let's get the right people on a call tomorrow."

Close"So: [amount] by [date], and I'll confirm by message now. Thank you, {{client}} — I appreciate you picking up."

Stage 06

Deliver — keep the client warm while you build

Goal: no surprises. The client hears from you before they wonder, and every "need from you" has a date.

Stage 07

Upsell, cross-sell & renew

Goal: every upsell starts with something you noticed in their data. Never "we also sell" — always "I saw this, here is what it means".

Quarterly review — the upsell conversation

Scorecard"Here's what we promised, and here's what happened: quote turnaround [before → after], margin visible per job, zero MyInvois rejections.enquiries per month [before → after], cost per lead, and which channel each came from. Where do you feel it most?"

Observation"One thing I noticed: [the data point]. That usually means [the implication]."

Offer"The natural next step is {{module}} — [one-line benefit]. It's [n] weeks on top of what you have, RM {{amount}}. Want me to scope it properly, or is there something more urgent I'm not seeing?"

If "not now""Fair. Shall I park it and raise it again at the next review, or is it a never?"

Cross-sell line"Your system can now handle three times the enquiries your website sends it. That's a marketing problem, not a system one — it's the other half of what we do, and the audit is free.""Enquiries are up — and half of them are landing in WhatsApp with no follow-up trail. That's a CRM problem, and we can wire the site straight into Odoo."

Stage 08

Refer, review & keep warm

Goal: turn a happy client into the next lead. Ask at the moment of delight, not at renewal.

Before you send anything

Read it aloud once. If it sounds like a template, it is one — change the first line. Then log it in the CRM.
